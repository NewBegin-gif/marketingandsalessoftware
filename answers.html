ActiveCampaign

Is ActiveCampaign worth it?

ActiveCampaign is genuinely best for businesses that want deep marketing automation and lifecycle email tied to a light CRM, and will actually use the automation depth. Skip it if you just need to send a newsletter — a simpler, cheaper email tool does that without paying for automation you won't touch.

How much does ActiveCampaign cost?

(re-verified 2026-08-17 in the browser; the figures below are the 'starts at' prices on annual billing, before you pick a contact count) No free plan, trial only. Four tiers: Starter from $15/mo with 1 user, limited segmentation and 5 actions per automation; Plus from $49/mo with 1 user, standard segmentation, unlimited automation actions and landing pages; Pro from $79/mo with 3 users, advanced segmentation, predictive and conditional content and attribution tracking; Enterprise from $145/mo with 5 users, premium segmentation, SSO, custom objects and a dedicated account team. Your email send allowance is a multiple of your contact count — 10x on Starter and Plus, 12x on Pro, 15x on Enterprise. The bill then scales with list size: the per-contact ladder only renders after you select a contact count, which is why we could not re-verify our earlier reading of about $189/mo for Plus at 10,000 contacts on this pass. CRM pipelines, SMS and extra users remain paid add-ons. Always verify the live price.

What is the catch with ActiveCampaign?

Pricing climbs with contact count and the add-ons (CRM, SMS, extra seats) stack up fast — the sticker Starter price is not what a serious automation setup actually costs. Powerful, but budget for the tier (Plus and up) you'll really use.

AltText.ai is genuinely best for image-heavy sites that need alt text written back into the CMS automatically — e-commerce catalogues, publishers and long-running blogs on WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Contentful, DatoCMS or Storyblok — where the native integrations, page crawler, CSV import/export and developer API let you clear thousands of missing alt attributes without touching them by hand. How much does AltText.ai cost?

(verified 21 Jul 2026) Credit-metered — one credit = one image. Free trial: 25 credits, no credit card required. Monthly: Bronze $5/mo (100 credits/mo, 5¢ per image), Silver $19/mo (500), Gold $59/mo (2,000), Titanium $119/mo (5,000), Platinum $229/mo (10,000). Annual buys the same yearly credit volume at a discount the page labels 17-31% off: $49/yr (1,200 credits), $189/yr (6,000), $489/yr (24,000), $1,179/yr (60,000), $2,199/yr (120,000). What is the catch with AltText.ai?

The headline ‘5¢ per image’ only holds for a plain JPG in one language. Those advanced formats are also locked out of the 25-credit free trial, so the one thing you cannot measure before paying is your own burn rate. Credits are spent on generation rather than on acceptance: regenerate a line you didn’t like and you pay again, and AltText.ai states plainly that used credits are not normally refundable. The category caveat an honest review owes you: models describe what is visibly in a photo well, but alt text is a judgement call — whether an image is decorative or informative, and what your brand actually calls the thing in it, is context the model doesn’t have. Bulk generation is a far better starting point than the empty alt attributes on most sites, but treat the output as a draft to spot-check. ‘ADA compliance’ here means the field gets filled, not a legal guarantee that what’s in it is right.
