We tested it · 2026-10-29

Tested by our team on 29 October 2026 on AWeber Free: we created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in our browser. We tried to import two made-up subscribers from a CSV and opened the Lite trial checkout without paying. AWeber refused both test addresses, so we stopped there.

How we tested

  1. Tried to import two made-up subscribers from a CSV
  2. Checked the list confirmation settings
  3. Opened the Lite trial checkout and stopped before entering a card
  • AWeber rejected both of our made-up addresses (on the reserved test domain example.com) as 'Blocked or invalid email format', so 0 of 2 were added. Following our test plan, we did not swap in real addresses; export and re-import were therefore not tested.
  • Before the import, AWeber asks how the subscribers joined your list (website, in person, bought a product, purchased the list or other) and whether they come from another provider. The 'Other' answer needs a written explanation.
  • Confirmation emails for imports are off by default, and the import itself defaults to 'No, they do not need to confirm'. Confirmation for subscribers added through the API is locked on; only AWeber Plus can ask to switch it off.

When the paid plan is worth it

AWeber still has a free tier, but it is no longer shown on the pricing page; you reach it through the separate free signup page, which documents "Up to 500 subscribers" and "3,000 emails per month". Those are the only two allowance figures that page prints, and neither is expressed as a multiple of your list. The paid cards, by contrast, promise sends of 10x your subscriber maximum on Lite and 12x on Plus.

Lite ($12.49/month billed annually, $15.00 month-to-month at 500 subscribers) holds you to 1 email list, 3 landing pages, 3 email automations, 3 users and 1 custom segment. Plus ($19.99/month annually, $30.00 monthly at 500) removes those caps and cuts the ecommerce transaction fee from 1.0% to 0.6%. Behavioural automation, webpage tracking and the free migration service are marked as excluded from Lite in AWeber's own comparison table, so if automation is the reason you are paying, Lite is the wrong stop.

Upgrading does not decouple price from list size: at 25,000 subscribers the month-to-month cost is $210.00 for Lite and $250.00 for Plus.

    How to actually use AWeber

    Signup collects your company name, website, industry and a postal address (AWeber notes this is required by CAN-SPAM), then asks whether you have subscribers to migrate. The real work is under Domains & Addresses: add a sending address and click the link in AWeber's verification email, then select it as your From address under List Settings. The docs warn against a generic address such as name@gmail.com. Next, authenticate the domain: CNAME records for DKIM plus a TXT record for DMARC.

    AWeber offers automatic setup for over 40 domain providers; otherwise you paste records into your DNS by hand and, per the docs, allow up to 48 hours for the records to propagate. This step bites later: AWeber re-checks sender authentication at send time, and a broadcast whose domain no longer validates is returned to Draft status. Second snag if you are migrating: imports run through a permission check and can be pulled for manual review.

    AWeber says such imports are typically reviewed within 1 business day (their claim, not our timing), until then nothing sends.

    Four of them are named on AWeber's own pricing page, read on 9 September 2026: an AI Page Builder for landing pages, an AI Signup Form Builder, an AI Writing Assistant for email copy and an AI Subject Line Assistant. They are presented as part of the product rather than as a separate paid add-on, and they sit alongside the ChatGPT plugin and Claude connector AWeber shipped in August 2026. Our 29 October 2026 test covered signup, import and the list, not these AI tools, so their output quality is not measured here: treat this as a list of what exists, not a verdict on how good it is. If you are choosing between AWeber and a cheaper sender on price alone, this is the part of the bundle that does not show up in a price comparison.

    Is an AWeber upgrade prorated, and do I get credit when I downgrade?

    Neither. AWeber's doc "Can I change billing plans later?" (docs.aweber.com, read 19 November 2026) says upgrades take effect immediately and are not pro-rated: the new plan is billed at its full price at the moment of upgrade, monthly to annual included. Downgrades to a lower plan or subscriber tier start at the next billing cycle (on annual billing, at the next monthly billing date), and include no refund or credit for the current period. The subscriber slider only offers tiers that fit your current count, so to drop a tier you first delete subscribers until the list fits.

    Can I pause AWeber instead of cancelling, and what can I take with me?

    Yes. Per "How do I cancel my account online?" (read 19 November 2026), a Hold Package costs $4.99 a month: subscribers, broadcasts, automations and landing pages are stored, but sending stops and no new subscribers are collected; it starts at the end of the paid term and any open balance must be paid first. To leave, use Close My Account on the billing page or live chat. Before that, the Backup Data feature emails a zip of all subscribers and messages, or export each list as CSV from the Subscribers page. User seats differ by plan: Free is owner only, Lite allows 3 users including the owner, Plus is unlimited.
