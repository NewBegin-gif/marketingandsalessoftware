What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Re-read on 24 July 2026, their homepage says 529 billion backlinks, their data hub says 1.1 trillion, and their backlinks page says 1.7 trillion in the prose while its own spec block on that same page says 1.1 trillion. The keyword count reads 8.62 billion on one page and 7.46 billion on another. That is not evidence the data is thin; it is evidence that these numbers cannot serve as a basis for your decision.

Judge it on your own searches during the trial instead. Two other things to weigh. Backlink reports are capped at 10,000 results per report on Team, Team x2 and Agency alike, so paying from $129 to $499 buys more of everything except a deeper backlink report. And 'maximum pages to scan' in a single crawl (5,000 / 25,000 / 50,000) is a far smaller number than the monthly 'pages to audit' allowance it sits next to.

When the paid plan is worth it

Individual at $69 monthly (or $50 annually) is a genuine working plan for one person, but two ceilings decide whether it holds: 100 searches a day, and no API access at all. If either binds you, the floor is Team at $129 monthly: that is where the API, unlimited projects and JavaScript rendering in the site audit start. Watch the middle step there: two dropdowns inside Team (more position checks, more API credits) push it to $169 monthly, which is within $40 of Team x2 at $209, so price the upgrade you actually need against the next tier before you pick.

Above that it is mostly volume: Team x2 doubles the seats to six, Agency takes you to thirty. What no tier fixes is the backlink report ceiling of 10,000 rows, identical on Team, Team x2 and Agency.

How to actually use Serpstat

Treat the trial as a subscription with a seven-day escape hatch, not as a free look. Serpstat's own FAQ says it takes a card, charges $1 to verify and returns it within 5 to 30 minutes. It turns on auto-renewal when the trial starts, and bills the plan automatically after seven days unless you cancel. Put that date in your calendar before you do anything else. Then spend the week on the only question that matters.

Run your own keywords, in your own country, and compare what comes back against what you already know to be true: that tells you more than any published database size, especially given those figures contradict each other. Watch the daily search counter while you do it; on Individual it stops at 100 a day, which is easy to hit in an afternoon of research and is the limit most likely to force you up a tier.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price on the page the price I will pay each month?

Only if you pay for a year up front. Re-read on 2 November 2026, the plan cards render the annual view and nothing else ($50, $100, $169 and $410 a month for Individual, Team, Team x2 and Agency) under a heading that says you save up to 27% with the annual discount. The month-to-month column is not on the page at all. What the page does print is the saving per plan: $228, $348, $480 and $1,068 a year (Team x2 read $484 in September). Divide those by twelve and add them back and you get the real month-to-month rates of roughly $69, $129, $209 and $499. So the gap between the two ways of paying is about a fifth to a quarter of the bill, and the number you are reading is the lower one. Budget from the figure you will actually be billed, not from the card.

How many people can use one subscription?

Fewer than you would guess, and you cannot buy your way around it a seat at a time. The comparison table, read 19 September 2026, puts an em dash under multi-user access on Individual, it is a single-user plan, then 3 users on Team, 6 on Team x2 and 30 on Agency. No per-seat price is published anywhere on the page. That makes the fourth person on a Team plan an upgrade rather than an add-on: $100 a month becomes $169, and the seventh person makes it $410. Serpstat's own FAQ points small agencies at Team and names the three-member limit in the same breath. Count the people who will log in before you compare this with anything else, because on headcount the cheap tier stops being cheap in one step.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You are billed unless you cancel, and the trial takes a card to start. Serpstat's own pricing FAQ, read 19 September 2026, is unusually direct about it: the trial runs seven days and gives you Individual or Team access; you enter a card for verification, which charges $1 and returns it to your account in five to thirty minutes; and, their wording, after initiating a free trial plan, auto-renewal of the plan is automatically activated, so if the renewal is not cancelled the plan amount is withdrawn after seven days. That is a trial that converts by default rather than one that lapses. Put the cancellation date in your calendar on day one, and use the week on your own keywords: seven days is enough to judge the data, and the bill arrives whether you did or not.

Can I connect Serpstat to ChatGPT or Claude?

From Team up, per the pricing page. The Team and Team x2 cards (re-read 2 November 2026) list 'API, ChatGPT and Claude Integration (MCP)' among the extended features; Individual has neither the API nor the integration. We have not connected it ourselves, and the cards do not say whether MCP calls draw on the same API credits; not confirmed.
