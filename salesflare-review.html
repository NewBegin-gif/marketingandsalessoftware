When the paid plan is worth it

Salesflare's plans start after a 30-day trial that requires no credit card; there is no permanently free tier. Solo users or small teams evaluating basic contact and email tracking can run the entire trial on any plan since core CRM functions (automated data capture, email tracking, mobile and LinkedIn access) are already present in the entry-level Growth plan ($39/month, or $29/month billed annually). The concrete push toward Pro ($64/month, $49 annual) is the lead credit allotment: Growth includes 5 lead credits per month, versus 100 on Pro and 250 on Enterprise, and multi-step email sequences and user permission controls are Pro-only.

What upgrading does not solve is capacity: Salesflare states there are no hidden limits on contacts, users, templates, or pipelines on any paid plan, so moving to a higher tier does not buy more storage or seats. Running out of lead credits is addressed by purchasing separate credit packs (from $39 for 250 on the pricing page; the help article says $49 on monthly billing), not by the plan tier itself beyond the monthly allotment.

Against the two CRMs it is most often weighed with, the per-seat arithmetic is close. Pipedrive's Growth plan is €39 per seat a month billed annually and €49 monthly, excluding VAT (read 17 October 2026); folk's Standard is $24 per member a month billed yearly or $30 monthly (read 19 September 2026). Salesflare's Growth at $29 or $39 sits between them. The difference shows up in permissions: on Salesflare they start at Pro, so a five-person team that needs reps restricted to their own records pays $245 a month on annual billing rather than $145.

    How to actually use Salesflare

    Initial setup means installing the Salesflare sidebar in your email inbox and, optionally, the Chrome extension for LinkedIn. Then connect your email account under Settings > Email Settings so contact capture, email tracking and signature sync start working automatically; the mobile app is a separate install. Existing contacts come in through Settings > Import Data as a CSV or from another database. Because Salesflare merges any re-imported contact with a matching email address into the existing record instead of creating a duplicate, messy or duplicate-heavy source files need cleaning before import or contacts silently merge into the wrong record.

    A common stall point is expecting full functionality from the trial. Multi-step email sequences and user permission controls are gated to the Pro plan. So a team trialling or buying the entry-level Growth plan will not see those features, even though the rest of the CRM is fully automated from day one. Since the 17 June 2026 update the importer proposes the column mapping itself from your headers and a sample of rows, recognises most date formats, and asks you to choose when a date such as 12/06/2026 could be read two ways; answer that prompt rather than accepting a default.

    Enterprise is the only card that lists 'Data migration done for you', and with its five-seat minimum that service starts at $495 a month billed annually. Each user can switch the interface language and dark mode under Settings > Regional settings, both added on 19 March 2026 along with the AI email assistant behind the Assist button.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Salesflare?

    Exit check 2026-10-29; route excerpt: Not tested: stopped at signup/onboarding under the connection and identity limits of the test plan; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    Can I cancel Salesflare at any time?

    Yes. The pricing FAQ (read 27 September 2026) says you can cancel whenever you want and stop being charged immediately, with no multi-year contract and no notice period before renewal. The terms of use (read 16 November 2026) say only that charges paid for service after termination are refunded 'starting from the next monthly or annual renewal', so the period you are in is not refunded. If that matters, weigh it before choosing the annual rate, which is $10 to $25 per user a month cheaper.

    What happens to my data when the Salesflare trial ends?

    It stays. The trial runs 30 days with no credit card, and the FAQ (read 27 September 2026) says Salesflare emails you before it ends; if you then pay, you keep the same account and everything you added during the trial rather than starting a new one. Pick the plan with care: sequences and user permissions are Pro-only, at $49 per user a month billed annually.

    Does Salesflare charge for onboarding or extra lead credits?

    Onboarding is free; extra lead credits are not. The FAQ (read 27 September 2026) promises no onboarding fee and offers a one-on-one training session for the team on request. Lead credits come with the plan, 5 a month on Growth, 100 on Pro and 250 on Enterprise, and more are sold as monthly packs. The pricing page lists 250 for $39, 500 for $69 and 1,000 for $129 without naming a billing term (read 12 November 2026). The help article (updated 3 July 2026) gives those as the yearly rates and lists $49, $79 and $139 for monthly billing. Enterprise also needs at least five users, so it starts at $495 a month billed annually.

    What do extra Salesflare lead credits really cost?

    Two Salesflare pages disagree. The pricing page, read 21 October 2026, prints the packs on every plan card as 250 credits for $39, 500 for $69 and 1,000 for $129, even under the euro prices. The help article on buying more lead credits (last updated 3 July 2026) lists those three figures as the per-month cost on yearly billing and gives $49, $79 and $139 on monthly billing, plus two larger packs: 3,000 credits for $399 a month or $349 yearly, and 5,000 for $549 or $499. Credits are pooled for the whole team, reset on your billing day, do not roll over, and pack changes are not prorated: an upgrade is charged at once and a downgrade does not credit earlier payments.

    Can a Salesflare rep be limited to their own accounts and deals?

    Yes, but only from Pro. The help article on visibility (updated 15 January 2026) sets it up in three places: Settings > Set Permissions, where View Data is switched from 'Team data' to 'Own data' or 'Group data', with the same choice for create, edit and delete; Settings > Manage Your Team, where users are dragged into groups; and Settings > Configure Pipelines, where each pipeline is opened to chosen groups. The four roles are Admin, Manager, Team Member and Viewer, and Viewers are read-only by default. The pricing page lists user permissions, view-only users and manager groups on the Pro card, so a Growth team at $29 a user cannot do any of this.

    Is there really no limit on contacts or users in Salesflare?

    No hard limit, with one reservation. The help article on record limits (updated 3 July 2026) says there is no cap on contacts, accounts or opportunities, but that Salesflare reserves the right to step in when an account adds more contacts than makes sense in a sales CRM, to avoid misuse and excessive costs. Users are also unlimited; each extra user is billed at your plan's per-user rate, and the next total appears under Settings > Billing & Usage > Overview.

    Can Salesflare be used from ChatGPT or Claude?

    Through an MCP server announced in the 17 June 2026 product update, which lets an AI assistant look up and work with your CRM data when you ask it to; the post links a setup guide and says official connectors for Claude and ChatGPT are still coming. The same release added bulk creation of opportunities from a filtered account list and of accounts from a contact list. Neither the post nor the pricing page says which plans include the MCP access.

    Can Salesflare change what I pay?

    Yes, with notice. Salesflare's terms of use (read 16 November 2026; no date on the page) let it vary any element of the charges by giving you written notice. Invoices are issued in advance of the period they cover and are due on issue; amounts are exclusive of VAT, so a European company sees VAT added on top of the per-user price. Overdue amounts can carry 12% interest a year, and support can be suspended while a payment is overdue.
