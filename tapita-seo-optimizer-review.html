What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The same Pro plan is $39.99 on Shopify Basic, $44.99 on Grow, $69.99 on Advanced and $99.99 on Shopify Plus, because Tapita adds $5, $30 or $60 on top depending on your Shopify plan. That is a two-and-a-half-fold spread on identical software, and it is the single largest factor in what this app costs you. Work out which row you are on before you compare it to anything else. The second meter has no published rate at all.

Basic gives you 100 AI metadata credits a month and Pro gives you 500, and beyond that the listing says only that more audit pages and more credits carry an extra charge. It does not say how much, and it does not say how many credits one product's metadata consumes. Tapita's own sister app publishes $0.06 a credit on its listing; this one publishes nothing, so you cannot price a month of AI metadata before you install.

And the free tier is a demonstration rather than a plan: 50 products for bulk alt text and file names, 30 images a day, and the SEO audit and LLMs.txt limited to basic pages. Any real catalogue is on a paid tier within a week, which is fine as long as you know that going in.

Unboxme (United States, about five hours, 14 July 2024) reports a homepage broken by the speed feature that neither disabling the feature nor removing the app resolved; the vendor attributes the missing CSS file to the merchant's own team. Where the ratings disagree. The complaint that reviewers do make and the cost driver that actually decides the bill are not the same thing. Reviewers say the app is expensive, but none of the reviews read here — the first page plus the full set of sub-five-star reviews the listing filter returns — connects that to which Shopify plan the store is on, even though the same Pro plan is priced differently on Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus. Second, read the tenures next to the praise: about two hours, one day, ten days, 24 days. That is long enough to judge a support conversation and too early to judge whether a schema or speed change moved anything in search, which is what the app is bought for.

Your Shopify plan sets the price, not the tier

The number on the listing is a Shopify-Basic number, and the line underneath it is what actually decides your bill. Tapita adds a surcharge tied to your Shopify plan: +$5 on Grow, +$30 on Advanced and +$60 on Shopify Plus. So Basic is $9.99, $14.99, $39.99 or $69.99, and Pro is $39.99, $44.99, $69.99 or $99.99, for identical software. Two consequences follow, and the second one is a genuine buying rule.

First, the spread is two and a half fold: Pro on Shopify Plus costs $99.99 against $39.99 on Shopify Basic. A Plus merchant on Tapita's *cheapest* tier pays $69.99 — more than a Shopify Basic merchant pays for Tapita's most expensive one. Second, because the surcharge is charged per install rather than per tier, the gap between Basic and Pro is a flat $30 whatever Shopify plan you are on.

That changes the upgrade decision completely depending on where you sit. On Shopify Basic, Pro costs four times what Basic does and needs real justification. On Shopify Plus, Pro costs only 43% more than Basic — $99.99 against $69.99 — for five times the AI credits, five times the daily image allowance, unlimited keyword research and hands-on help with Search Console issues. If you are on Plus or Advanced, take Pro; the relative cost of the upgrade has already been paid.

The second meter has no published rate at all. Basic includes 100 AI metadata credits a month and Pro 500, and beyond that the listing says only that extra audit pages and extra credits carry a charge. It does not say how much, and it does not say how many credits one product's metadata consumes — so you cannot price a month of AI metadata before you install. Tapita's own sister app publishes $0.06 a credit; if that rate carries across, the 100 credits on Basic are about $6 of the $9.99 plan, which would make this mostly a credit purchase.

Treat that as an inference from a neighbouring listing, not a quoted price, and ask. Annual billing saves 15% — $101.88 for Basic against $119.88, and $408 for Pro against $479.88 — though it is not stated whether those annual figures include the Shopify-plan surcharge. If you are on Advanced or Plus, get that confirmed before you prepay. Find your row before you compare this app to anything. Then let that decide your tier rather than your feature list. Because the surcharge is per install, the step from Basic to Pro is a flat $30 whatever Shopify plan you are on: four times the price on Shopify Basic, but only 43% more on Plus.

On Advanced or Plus, take Pro — you have already paid the expensive part. Ask two questions before you subscribe. How many AI credits one product's metadata consumes, and what an extra credit costs — the listing publishes neither, so a month of AI metadata is unpriceable in advance. Use the free tier to check quality on a sample. Fifty products for bulk alt text is enough to see whether the generated metadata reads like your brand or like a template, which is the only thing worth testing before you pay.
