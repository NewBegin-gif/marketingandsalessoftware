What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It generates posts quickly and schedules them daily or weekly in bulk, and a Shopify blog filled with unedited AI articles is precisely the pattern search engines have spent the last two years demoting: the app lowers the cost of production, not the bar for ranking. Used the other way round, as a drafting and structuring tool for posts you actually edit, it is sensible. The price is the second thing to watch, and on 19 September 2026 there is none to read.

The listing publishes no plan block and gives its pricing as Free. That is not the same as free being the whole story: the features it advertises (AI generation, bulk creation, translation, scheduling) were metered in credits until August, and no credit allowance or overage rate is stated anywhere on the page now. The same developer's other listing still prints a complete ladder, so this one is not simply a developer who does not publish prices.

Go in expecting to ask what the limits are, and do not plan a content calendar around an allowance nobody has told you the size of.
