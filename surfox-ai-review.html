We tested it · 2026-10-12

A review account in SurFox's review sandbox, given to us by the founder. The sandbox says so on every page: leads, dashboards and results are sample data, messaging is switched off and the AI Learning page is a mock-up. Two things work for real there, the test conversation on a campaign and Surf, the campaign assistant. We played leads against a sample campaign for an analytics company over SMS on 3 October 2026, sent our findings to SurFox, and re-tested on 6 and 12 October 2026 after they shipped fixes. We did not send a real text, connect a CRM, take a voice call or see the learning feature work on real conversations.

  • It did not invent a price. Asked what the product costs, the AI said the price depends on data sources and users and would be given on the call, which matched the knowledge document we gave it. A request to stop texting was honoured at once and classed as an opt-out.
  • The first test, on 3 October, had visible faults: a placeholder [SLOTS] sent as text, a summary claiming the lead had picked a time slot when they had not, a question answered as unknown although the knowledge document covered it, and openers longer than the 150 characters SurFox aims for. SurFox fixed these after our feedback. On 12 October the AI offered three real times, the booking produced a hot-lead email with a calendar file and a 20-minute duration that matched the conversation, and two openers came in at 143 and 130 characters.
  • Asking for a call directly ('Can someone call me this week?') ended the test as a hand-off to the team without offering a time; asking to book a demo did offer times. Both are reasonable, but you choose which one your campaign should push.

Four monthly plans, priced by message volume rather than seats. Starter is $49 a month: 1,000 SMS messages, 100 website chat conversations and 20 voice minutes a month, up to 500 new SMS leads, 3 active campaigns and 1 user, with the core AI, knowledge-base upload and Google and Outlook calendar booking, but no conversation learning. Growth is $597 a month: 10,000 messages, 500 website chats, 400 voice minutes and 5 users, plus conversation learning from the last 100 conversations, A/B testing, Zapier and a Chrome extension for lead capture.

Growth Plus is $1,497 a month: 25,000 messages, 2,000 chats, 1,000 voice minutes, 15 users, learning from the last 1,000 conversations, multiple personas, unlimited knowledge-base uploads, white-glove onboarding and a dedicated success manager. Scale is $2,497 a month: 40,000 messages, 5,000 chats, 2,000 voice minutes and 15 or more users with the same features. A message is any text in the conversation, yours or the lead's; the page puts a conversation at about 4 to 8 messages.

Full pricing notes (2 more paragraphs)

When you near the message limit you are emailed and can buy a top-up, at a price the page does not publish. Both inbound and outbound calls count toward the voice minutes, and minutes over the bucket are billed automatically at $0.25 a minute. Website chats pause when the allowance runs out. There are no setup fees and no annual plans; the FAQ says annual options may come later, and the terms reserve annual billing for custom enterprise agreements.

You can cancel any time with 24 hours' notice, with no refund for a partial month. Every plan carries a 30-day money-back guarantee whose clock starts when your carrier (A2P) registration is approved, not at payment, and which pays out only if you sent at least 80% of your monthly messages and the AI demonstrably failed at its core tasks; the pricing page now states the 80% condition itself and adds “Conditions apply, see terms” with a link to the terms.

Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The money-back guarantee depends on using 80% of your messages and on SurFox verifying from your logs that the AI failed; since 16 October the pricing page says so too and links to the terms. Inbound and outbound calls both count as voice minutes, and overage is billed automatically. Message top-up prices are not published. The terms put all TCPA liability on you, with an uncapped indemnity, and forbid purchased or scraped lists.

We tested only in a sandbox with sample data, so the learning feature, delivery and voice are unverified by us.

What SurFox AI does

AI agent that texts, qualifies and books leads over SMS, website chat and inbound voice calls, then hands the ready ones to your team; aimed at staffing, real estate and home-services firms in the US.

The jump after Starter is twelvefold

Starter at $49 is a real trial of the product, not a demo: the same AI, calendar booking and knowledge base, on 1,000 messages a month. Because replies count too, and the vendor puts a conversation at 4 to 8 messages, that is roughly 125 to 250 conversations a month, our arithmetic. What Starter lacks is the feature SurFox sells hardest: conversation learning, where the AI studies past conversations and adjusts its style.

The next step is Growth at $597, twelve times the price for ten times the messages, plus learning from the last 100 conversations, A/B testing, Zapier and five users. That is the plan the product is built around, and it only pays if you have the volume: a list big enough to use most of 10,000 messages and a team that will take the calls. Growth Plus and Scale are volume tiers with the larger learning window of 1,000 conversations.

Watch voice on every plan: minutes over the bucket are charged automatically at $0.25 each, while message overages wait for you to buy a top-up.

How to actually use SurFox AI

Plan for the carrier registration first. US carriers require A2P registration before any business can send texts through SurFox, and the terms say you are charged at signup while your message allowance stays locked until that approval comes through. Carriers can ask for changes to your website, forms and consent wording, and the delay does not pause billing. Have your consent language and a clean, first-party contact list ready before you pay.

Then build one campaign with a knowledge document that answers the questions leads actually ask: price, contract terms, integrations, next steps. In our sandbox test the AI declined to make up a price that was not in the document and passed the question to the sales call, which is the behaviour you want. Use the built-in test conversation to play an interested lead, a no and a maybe before any real text goes out.

Start on Starter with a focused list. Only move to Growth when the volume and the learning feature are worth $597 a month to you, and keep an eye on voice minutes, which bill automatically past the bucket.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SurFox AI cost?

Read on 15 October 2026: Starter $49 a month for 1,000 SMS messages, 100 website chats and 20 voice minutes; Growth $597 for 10,000 messages and conversation learning; Growth Plus $1,497 for 25,000; Scale $2,497 for 40,000. Billing is monthly only, with no setup fee. Voice minutes over your bucket cost $0.25 each and are billed automatically.

Does SurFox AI really refund every dollar?

Only under conditions. The pricing page and the terms, both re-read 16 October 2026, require that you sent at least 80% of your monthly messages through your approved flow within 30 days of carrier approval, and that the AI demonstrably failed at responding, following your flow, booking or disqualifying on a significant portion of conversations. SurFox verifies both from your logs. Ordinary cancellation gives no refund for a partial month.

Do my texts count lead replies too?

Yes. SurFox counts every message in the conversation, your outbound texts and the lead's replies together, and says a conversation uses about 4 to 8 messages. Its founder told us the allowance counts messages, not SMS segments. So Starter's 1,000 messages is roughly 125 to 250 conversations a month, our arithmetic.
