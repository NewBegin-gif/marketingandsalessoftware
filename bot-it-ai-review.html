What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Pro's automated fixes draw on a monthly token budget the vendor describes only as generous. Its FAQ puts a typical product at about a thousand tokens to enrich once and says the app stops proposing, never mid-write, when you reach the ceiling. For a catalogue of a few hundred products that is unlikely to matter. For tens of thousands of SKUs, or for re-enriching after every catalogue change, ask for the number in writing before you rely on it.

It is a bet on a channel that is still small. The app prepares your catalogue for AI shopping agents, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, and tracks where your products place in their results. For most stores those agents are not yet a meaningful source of orders, so the rank tracker and the agent-traffic analytics may show very little for a while. The audit is useful regardless, because the fields it checks (categories, attributes, barcodes, alt text, variants, policies) also feed shopping feeds and on-site search.

Nobody has reviewed it yet. The app launched on the Shopify App Store on 8 May 2026 and still had zero reviews on 24 September 2026, so there is no outside account of how accurate its generated attributes are. Start in Full Review mode, where every proposal waits for your approval, before you let Full Auto write to your live catalogue.

What Bot It AI does

Shopify app that audits every product for the data AI shopping agents rank on (categories, attributes, barcodes, alt text, variants, policies), maintains llms.txt and agents.md, and on Pro writes the missing fields with one-click revert and tracks placement in AI shopping results. Free audit forever; Pro $29 a month with a 30-day trial. Launched May 2026, no reviews yet. Read 24 Sep 2026.

Free diagnoses, Pro repairs

The line between the two tiers is clean. On Free you get the complete audit, with every product scored on 13 checks, the list of failing products and the store-level gates check, plus the llms.txt, agents.md and robots.txt theme files, the brand voice editor and unlimited narratives. What Free does not do is write to your catalogue: category-attribute proposals are shown rather than applied, and alt text and SEO fields stay as they are.

So the $29 buys labour, and whether it is worth it depends on the size of the gap the free audit finds. If it lists a few dozen products missing alt text, writing them yourself takes an afternoon. If it lists thousands of missing attributes and barcodes, $29 a month for proposals you approve in bulk is cheap. The vendor's FAQ says the changes are written as ordinary Shopify product fields, metafields and metaobjects that stay if you uninstall, so you can run Pro for a month or two, clear the backlog and drop back to Free without losing the work.

The 30-day trial makes that cheaper still: cancel before it ends and nothing is charged, and you keep every audit result. What keeps Pro worth paying for after the backlog is gone are the parts that only run while you subscribe, namely the nightly rank tracker, the IndexNow broadcasts, the enrichment proxy and the analytics.

How to actually use Bot It AI

Install from the Shopify App Store and run the free audit first. The vendor's documentation promises a store discoverable by AI agents in under 15 minutes; what you actually get in that time is the Agent Readiness Score and a list of failing products and checks, which is the useful part. Fill in the brand voice editor next, with your brand summary, tone, shipping policies and FAQs, because the generated narratives and fixes draw on it.

If the audit shows a real backlog, start the 30-day Pro trial and set automation to Full Review, where every proposal waits for your approval. Read the first batch carefully: generated attributes and alt text are only as accurate as the product data behind them. Move to Semi Auto once the proposals are consistently right, and watch the Automation tab, which shows your token usage against the monthly ceiling. Every change is logged and revertible in one click, and the changes stay in your store if you later uninstall.
