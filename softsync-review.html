What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The sixth person does not add a sixth Pro seat; it moves the whole team to Business, which is $59 per seat a month on yearly billing instead of $29. A five-person team on Pro pays $145 a month billed yearly; the same team plus one hire pays $354 on Business, for the same product surface, since the pricing page says both paid plans carry the same core features. If your team is near that line, price the Business plan now rather than after you have moved your pipeline in.

On Pro the monthly allowances are 1,000 campaign emails and 1,000 AI chats per user, 1,000 enrichment credits for the whole workspace (per workspace on the table re-read 11 November 2026), and one connected mailbox per user. A single rep running cold outreach from SoftSync can reach 1,000 campaign emails well before the month ends, and the page does not say what happens when you do, whether sending stops or an overage is charged.

Ask before you plan a campaign around it. Month-to-month billing costs about a third more than yearly: $39 against $29 on Pro, $79 against $59 on Business. There is no free plan, and the pricing page does not state a trial length; it says the checkout shows any trial terms. SoftSync's own home page says it has no integrations directory and instead gives your AI agent a way in through the API and MCP access; if your stack depends on ready-made connectors, check the specific ones on the integrations page before you commit.

We found no independent buyer reviews we could read, so the only voices on the site are the testimonials SoftSync chose, most of them from founders and investors rather than daily users.

What SoftSync does

Flexible B2B CRM for small sales teams: records and custom objects, table, pipeline and calendar views, email sync and campaigns, meeting notes and summaries, Autopilot follow-up workflows, and API and MCP access so Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini can work with the CRM directly.

Business buys room, not features

The pricing page is explicit that Pro and Business run the same product: the same records, views, email sync, campaigns, meeting summaries, reports, AI Assistant, Autopilot, integrations, API and MCP access. Business costs twice as much per seat, $59 against $29 billed yearly, and what it buys is room and help. The room is the reason most teams will move. Pro caps a workspace at five team members and ten groups, 250,000 records per table and one mailbox per user, with 1,000 campaign emails and chats per user and 1,000 enrichment credits per workspace each month.

Business allows 25 team members, 100 groups, a million records per table, five mailboxes per user and 10,000 of each monthly allowance. The help is Priority Support for data migration and a workspace consultant, both self-served on Pro. So the decision is arithmetic, not taste. A team of five or fewer that sends a few hundred campaign emails per rep a month has no reason to leave Pro. A sixth user, a second mailbox per rep, or outbound volume above 1,000 emails per person a month each force Business on their own, and at that point the whole team pays the higher rate.

Enterprise is for capacity beyond 25 people or custom limits and is priced on request.

    Can you export your data from SoftSync?

    Exit check 2026-11-18; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    What does SoftSync cost?

    Pro is $29 per seat a month billed yearly or $39 billed monthly; Business is $59 per seat a month yearly or $79 monthly; Enterprise is custom-priced. Prices exclude VAT. There is no free plan. Read on softsync.ai/pricing on 26 September 2026.

    What is the difference between Pro and Business?

    Capacity and support, not features. Pro allows up to 5 team members, 1 mailbox per user, 1,000 campaign emails and chats per user a month and 1,000 enrichment credits for the workspace; Business allows 25 team members, 5 mailboxes per user and 10,000 of each, plus Priority Support for migration and a workspace consultant.

    Does SoftSync work with Claude or ChatGPT?

    SoftSync says so on its home page: it connects to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, and both paid plans include API and MCP access. It also says it has no integrations directory and gives your AI agent a way in instead.

    Does SoftSync put a bot in my meetings?

    No. SoftSync's home page (read 6 October 2026) says the meeting notepad listens from your computer, with no bot on the call, and that the summary and next steps are added to the customer's record; the desktop recorder is listed on both paid plans. SoftSync's COO told us through PartnerStack on 6 October 2026 that the recorder now runs on both Mac and Windows; the home page we read does not name the operating systems, so check that your machine is supported before a team-wide rollout.

    Does SoftSync have a SOC 2 report?

    No issued report is claimed. SoftSync's Trust Centre shows a SOC 2 Type II "Ready" badge, and its SOC 2 page at softsync.ai/trust/soc-2-type-ii, read 11 November 2026, says the badge describes alignment and does not represent an issued report. The ISO 27001 entry is likewise described as alignment. If your procurement checklist needs an auditor's SOC 2 report, ask SoftSync for its status in writing; the Trust Centre has a request-documentation link.
