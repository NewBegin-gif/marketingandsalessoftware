When the paid plan is worth it

Reditus has no free tier; the entry point is the $99/month Growth plan, offered with a 14-day trial that requires no credit card. Growth covers affiliate programs tracking up to $60K in ARR, includes one AI-assisted affiliate search per month, and gives access to what the pricing page calls a "limited" affiliate database — workable for a single company running one program without constant partner sourcing. The concrete push toward Scale Up ($399/month, billed annually) is that $60K ARR cap and the one-search-per-month limit: once tracked revenue or affiliate-discovery needs grow past those numbers, Growth stops covering the account.

What upgrading does not fix: Scale Up adds a marketplace listing and full access to the affiliate database, which raises exposure to Reditus's pool of affiliates, but it does not make affiliates apply, get approved, or drive sales. That still depends on the commission structure and recruiting effort a company puts in, neither of which is a paid-tier feature.

How to actually use Reditus

The first real step after signing up for the 14-day free trial is entering company details, which Reditus uses to generate a custom affiliate landing page and sign-up page. Next comes installation: a tracking script placed on every page of the main domain and on the sign-up page itself, plus a sign-up snippet (gr('track', 'conversion', {email: ...})) that must fire exactly once, on a successful new sign-up. Reditus's own installation guide flags the main failure point directly: placing the tracking script behind a cookie consent wall, which stops conversions from being recorded.

Payouts need Stripe connected, or manual handling through the Reditus API if Stripe isn't used. On the Growth plan, affiliate discovery is capped at one AI-assisted search per month against a database the pricing page calls "limited," so building out a partner roster beyond that search can stall until a Scale Up upgrade.

Frequently asked questions

What does Reditus cost?

The published entry is $99 a month billed annually — $1,188 once a year, which the page frames as four months free against monthly. That plan tracks up to $60K of ARR through your affiliate programme; the next tier goes to $360K. Read on 17 August 2026.

What does the payout processing fee add?

5% on card payouts and 2% on invoice, on every plan — that is the number to model rather than the subscription. If you pay out $5,000 a month to affiliates by card, the processing alone is $250, more than twice the entry subscription.

How do I know which tier I need?

By tracked ARR, not by seats or affiliates. The entry plan covers up to $60K and the next up to $360K. Because the tier names have changed over time, compare on that ARR ceiling when reading any older review — including ours before this update.
