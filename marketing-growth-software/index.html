Frequently asked questions

What's the best marketing software for a small business?

There's no single best — it depends on your channel. Email tools win for nurturing an owned audience, ad and SEO tools for acquisition, and social schedulers for consistency. Pick the channel where your customers already are, and get good at one before adding another.

Do I need a CRM if I'm just starting out?

Once you're juggling more than a handful of leads in a spreadsheet, yes. A lightweight CRM stops deals slipping through the cracks. Start simple — the best CRM is the one your team will actually keep updated.

Is there free marketing and growth software?

Plenty of tools here have free tiers or trials — email, CRM and social scheduling especially. Start free, prove it drives results, then pay only when a limit holds you back. Check each provider's current free limits, as they change.
