We tested it · 2026-11-06

Tested by our team on 6 November 2026 on the free trial: Daan created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in Daan's browser. We built one lead form, published it and sent one fictional test lead through it. We did not export the lead and did not open checkout.

How we tested

  1. Started a form from scratch and replaced the title and description with our test text
  2. Published the form and submitted one fictional lead in a separate tab
  3. Read the lead list, the counter and the plan screens
  • The plan page opens in the monthly view: Free at $0.00 with 5 leads a month and Unlimited at $4.99 a month. That matches our read of 18 October. The Free button did not respond, so we used Skip. No card was asked for.
  • The welcome screen says 'Your free trial of Tapform starts today' and shows decorative cards reading 'Payment successful'. Nothing was paid. Inside the app, a pop-up then says 'You are on free plan' and a banner repeats it, which does not fit a trial that had just started.
  • A pop-up offering a free call with a sales person appeared twice during the session.

What Tapform does

No-code builder for multi-step lead capture forms, quizzes and popups that you embed on any website, with lead notifications, tracking pixels and CRM integrations; a small free plan and an unlimited plan for agencies and growing sites.

Free is a test, not a plan

The two plans differ in volume first. Free stops at 5 leads a month on a single form, with one user and 100 MB of storage, so a site that gets even a couple of enquiries a week passes it in the first month. Unlimited removes the caps on leads, forms, workspaces, users, domains and storage. At $4.99 a month billed monthly, Unlimited is cheap enough that the plan question mostly answers itself once you have more than a handful of leads.

For agencies the unlimited workspaces and domains are the point: one subscription can carry forms for several clients. What we could not settle is the yearly price, because the yearly view shows a higher per-month figure than the monthly one. Start monthly until Tapform's checkout shows what a year actually costs.

What we found running Tapform ourselves

Hands-on · 2026-11-06

The first findings are at the top of this page. The rest:

  • The default form comes with a consent text under it. It says Tapform and its providers may contact you by text or call, some of it automated, once the form is submitted. Check that wording before you put the form on your own site.
  • Our title and description with the accent in 'Zoë', the quotation marks and the dash were saved and shown correctly. The published form carries a 'Powered by tapform' badge, and its public address is built from the account holder's full name.
  • The address block took only five-digit US-style ZIP codes. The field is numeric, so the Dutch postcode '3011 AB' was cut to '3011' and then refused with 'Zip must be 5 digit number!', although the country was set to the Netherlands. We typed '30110' to get past it. The lead arrived with that value.
  • After the submit, the thank-you screen carries an advert, 'Create your Tapform'. The lead list showed our lead and a demo lead that was already there. The counter read 'Leads collected 1/5', so the demo lead does not count.
  • The lead detail kept the accent and the quotation marks. We could not confirm from the text view that the line break in the notes field survived.

    How to actually use Tapform

    Start on Free or the 14-day trial and build one real form. Tapform describes the route on its homepage: build the form in the drag-and-drop editor, add multiple-choice, dropdown and other question types, and install it on your site with a copy-paste embed code. Send a few test submissions and check that the email notification arrives and that the lead lands where you want it; the pricing FAQ names HubSpot, Mailchimp, Zapier and Google Sheets among the integrations.

    If you take the trial, put day 14 in your calendar. The pricing page says you can fall back to Free when it ends, but the terms say billing starts automatically unless you cancel, so decide before then. If you pay and change your mind, the refund request has to go in within 14 calendar days of the first purchase.

    Distilled from Tapform's own pages: tapform.com/pricing, read in a browser on 18 October 2026 in the monthly and yearly view, and the homepage, the pricing FAQ and the terms of service at tapform.com/terms-of-service (trial billing and the 14-day refund clause), read on 15 October 2026. We found no independent setup guide worth citing. Tapform is an affiliate partner of ours through Lemon Squeezy.
