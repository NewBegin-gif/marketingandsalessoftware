lemlist: what it costs and who should skip it

Cold email outreach and sales engagement. Build personalized multichannel sequences (email, LinkedIn, calls) with built-in deliverability tools, and trigger them off buying-intent signals its agents surface. An MCP server at app.lemlist.com/mcp puts the same actions inside Claude or ChatGPT: create, update, start and pause campaigns and sequences, manage leads, enrich emails and read team analytics in plain language - the enrichment calls spend credits like anywhere else in the product.

What to check before you buy

The per-seat plus per-email-account model makes total cost unpredictable as you scale, and reviews flag support response times and the LinkedIn Chrome-extension's reliability.

The pricing, as we read it

(re-verified 2026-08-17; the page served us DOLLARS today where our previous reading was in euros, with the same numerals — check your own checkout) No free plan, 14-day trial. Email is $69/month, or $55/month on yearly billing, covering unlimited users and 50,000 emails a month.

Multichannel is $109/month per user, or $87/month yearly, with unlimited emails and messages and 5 senders per user. Quarterly billing saves 10% and yearly 20%. Enterprise (5+ users) is quote-only.

Contact data is billed separately through rechargeable credits where 1 credit = $0.01: a 1,000-credit pack is $10 and buys roughly 200 email lookups or 50 phone numbers, with a phone number costing 20 credits.

When the paid tier is worth it

lemlist has no free plan — you get 14 days of full Multichannel access, no credit card required. The real decision is Email (69€/month, 55€ on annual billing) versus Multichannel (109€/month, 87€ annual, billed per user).

Email covers 50,000 emails a month, unlimited users, the email and phone finder, and the 650M+ lead database. Multichannel lifts that cap to unlimited emails and messages, and adds LinkedIn, SMS and WhatsApp automation, a call dialer, and 5 senders per user.

Upgrade if you run LinkedIn or phone touches inside the same sequences as your email, or if you send more than 50,000 emails a month; below that volume the higher tier buys channels, not better email. The seat model also flips: Email is unlimited users, Multichannel is charged per user.

Upgrading does not change enrichment either, which stays pay-per-credit on every plan — 1,000 credits for 10€, 5 credits per email verification, 20 credits per phone number — and unused credits expire every month with no rollover. Saleshandy, which sells a competing sequencer, puts a five-person annual Multichannel team at $435/month listed.

It says the real cost can reach $700-800 once extra senders ($9/month each), credit top-ups and add-ons are counted. That same article closes by telling readers to start with Saleshandy. Check that figure against your own seat and credit use — it is a calculation by a competitor, not a measured bill.

How we would start

lemlist's setup guide lists three steps: connect your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, or custom SMTP), set up your email signature, then add SPF, DKIM and a custom tracking domain to your DNS. The guide estimates 15-30 minutes depending on DNS propagation, and notes records can take up to 48 hours to show as verified in your lemlist settings — do not launch before they do.

Where people get stuck: the review on Saleshandy's blog, written by a competing sequencer, found connecting email and LinkedIn accounts overly complicated for new users before their first campaign, and reports users periodically removing and reinstalling the LinkedIn Chrome extension. LinkedIn volume ramps automatically: newly connected accounts start at 2 invitations a day and increase by 2 per day up to your configured cap, with 20 a day the recommended setup.

Teams that get results build and verify lead lists in external tools, then import clean contacts.

Who it is for

Best for outbound teams that live in cold email and want multichannel sequences, warm-up and a big template community in one per-seat tool.

Skip it if you send occasional one-off campaigns — the entry plan starts at 69€/month and only pays off with steady outbound volume.

What to weigh it against

The comparison that matters is Instantly or Smartlead — cheaper, unlimited-mailbox cold-email tools.

How much does the contact data cost on top?

It is a separate credit balance where one credit is a cent: a $10 pack of 1,000 credits buys roughly 200 verified emails or 50 phone numbers, since a phone number costs 20 credits. That means data for a 1,000-contact campaign is a real line item alongside the seat, not an included extra.

Which plan covers LinkedIn and calls?

Multichannel, at $87 per user a month yearly. The Email plan is email only and unlimited-user, which makes it cheap for a team that just sends sequences; the moment you want LinkedIn steps the price becomes per user, which changes the arithmetic for anything larger than a couple of reps.
