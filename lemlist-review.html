What lemlist does

Sales outreach platform that finds target accounts, spots buying signals and enriches contacts, then runs personalized multichannel sequences (email, LinkedIn, calls) with built-in deliverability tools. An MCP server at app.lemlist.com/mcp puts the same actions inside Claude or ChatGPT: create, update, start and pause campaigns and sequences, manage leads, enrich emails and read team analytics in plain language - the enrichment calls spend credits like anywhere else in the product.

What buyers report about lemlist

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run lemlist ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Personalisation — the image and video variables in particular — is what reviewers name first, and it is genuinely distinctive. 4.6 on G2 across 1,500+ reviews, 76% five-star.
  • • Support responds fast, and the multichannel sequencing works as advertised.
What they complain about
  • • The credit system is where the bill goes wrong. Reviewers who signed up at $79 report averaging $150-$200 a month once credits, extra sending addresses and add-ons are counted — 30-50% above the headline.
  • • Enrichment credits (200 on Email Pro, 400 on Multichannel Expert) expire monthly with no rollover, and heavy prospectors exhaust them quickly.
  • • Dozens of reviewers flag it as expensive specifically at three or more seats.
  • • LinkedIn functionality is thinner than dedicated LinkedIn tools, and the HubSpot integration plus Chrome extension are both reported as unstable.

When the paid plan is worth it

lemlist has no free plan — you get 14 days of full Multichannel access, no credit card required. The real decision is Email ($69/month, $55 on annual billing) versus Multichannel ($109/month, $87 annual, billed per user). Email covers 50,000 emails a month, unlimited users, the email and phone finder, and the 650M+ lead database. Multichannel lifts that cap to unlimited emails and messages, and adds LinkedIn, SMS and WhatsApp automation, a call dialer, and 5 senders per user.

Upgrade if you run LinkedIn or phone touches inside the same sequences as your email, or if you send more than 50,000 emails a month; below that volume the higher tier buys channels, not better email. The seat model also flips: Email is unlimited users, Multichannel is charged per user. Upgrading does not change enrichment either: contact data is a separate monthly add-on on every plan — Lead data enrichment, 2,000 credits a month for €20 (€16 on yearly billing), about 400 emails or 100 phone numbers — and the pricing page now says unused credits roll over while the subscription stays active.

Saleshandy, which sells a competing sequencer, puts a five-person annual Multichannel team at $435/month listed. It says the real cost can reach $700-800 once extra senders ($9/month each), credit top-ups and add-ons are counted. That same article closes by telling readers to start with Saleshandy. Check that figure against your own seat and credit use — it is a calculation by a competitor, not a measured bill.

How to actually use lemlist

lemlist's setup guide lists three steps: connect your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, or custom SMTP), set up your email signature, then add SPF, DKIM and a custom tracking domain to your DNS. The guide estimates 15-30 minutes depending on DNS propagation, and notes records can take up to 48 hours to show as verified in your lemlist settings — do not launch before they do.

Frequently asked questions

What does lemlist cost?

Email is $69 a month, or $55 on yearly billing, with unlimited users and 50,000 emails a month. Multichannel is $109 per user a month, or $87 yearly. Paying quarterly takes 10% off the bill and paying yearly 20%. Read on 17 August 2026 — and note the same numerals appeared in euros on an earlier reading, so confirm your currency.

How much does the contact data cost on top?

It is a separate monthly add-on: Lead data enrichment costs €20 a month, or €16 on yearly billing, for 2,000 credits, about 400 emails or 100 phone numbers, and unused credits roll over while the subscription runs. Buying-intent signals are a second add-on at €29 a month (€21 yearly) for 100 signals. Read on 5 October 2026 in euros from the Netherlands. Data for a 1,000-contact campaign is a real line item alongside the seat, not an included extra.

Which plan covers LinkedIn and calls?

Multichannel, at $87 per user a month yearly. The Email plan is email only and unlimited-user, which makes it cheap for a team that just sends sequences; the moment you want LinkedIn steps the price becomes per user, which changes the arithmetic for anything larger than a couple of reps.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
