The honest knock

The AI visibility tracking is marked Beta. In our test (18 Nov 2026) the free SEO Checker reported "No additional page markup was found" on a page that carries 3 JSON-LD blocks. We have not paid for a plan, so the full site audit is untested by us.

What Seobility does

SEO software suite with website audit, rank monitoring, backlink monitoring, uptime checks and AI visibility tracking, with a free Basic account and a 14-day trial on paid plans.

Premium lifts 10 keywords to 300 and adds backlink monitoring

Basic limits you to 1 project, 10 keywords and 100 pages per crawl. Premium raises that to 3 projects, 300 keywords and 25,000 pages per crawl, and adds backlink monitoring and uptime checks. Professional and Agency add projects, keywords, white label reports and, on Agency, phone support. For one small site, the free account shows whether the audit is useful. Premium is the step when you need more than ten keywords.

What no plan solves is a very large site: the FAQ (read 17 November 2026) caps a crawl at 100,000 subpages per project, which is the Agency limit, so a bigger site is never audited in full. Every paid plan renews monthly or yearly and can be cancelled at any time without a notice period.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Seobility?

    Exit check 2026-11-18; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does Seobility cost?

    Per the pricing page read on 18 November 2026, in euros plus VAT: Premium is 59.90 a month, Professional 99.90 and Agency 209.90. Paying annually saves 20%, which makes the yearly totals 575.04, 959.04 and 2,015.04. A free Basic account has no time limit. The same page has a US dollar view at $69.90, $109.90 and $239.90 a month, and the free SEO Checker page quotes those dollar prices.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes, 14 days on the paid plans. The trial button on the free SEO Checker page (read 18 November 2026) says the trial automatically renews to the monthly Premium plan, at $69.90 a month on that page. There is also a free Basic account, with no card and no time limit.

    How many free checks do I get a day?

    The SEO Checker page (read 18 November 2026) lists 5 checks a day on the free Basic account, 50 on Premium, 100 on Professional and 200 on Agency, and the pricing table counts the requests across all the standalone tools. In our test (18 Nov 2026), without an account the checker stopped after 3 checks that day, shared across the free tools.

    What does the free plan include?

    One website project, 10 tracked keywords, 5 daily tool credits, a limited website audit, 100 pages per crawl and 3 AI prompts tracked.

    Can I cancel any time?

    The page says you can cancel any time without a notice period. Monthly contracts renew for a month and yearly contracts renew for a year.

    Does it track AI search?

    The pricing page lists AI Visibility Tracking, marked Beta: 15 prompts on 1 platform on Premium, 50 prompts on 2 platforms on Professional and 250 prompts on 2 platforms on Agency.

    What does extra capacity cost?

    Additional projects cost 4.90, 9.90 or 14.90 EUR a month depending on plan, and 100 extra keywords cost 6.90, 8.90 or 10.90 EUR a month.

    What do we not know?

    How accurate the crawl and rank data are, how long a full audit takes on a large site, and how support responds. We have not paid for a plan.

    Do I need a card for the 14-day trial of a paid plan?

    Yes. The pricing FAQ (read 17 November 2026) says Seobility asks for a valid payment method at signup and charges nothing if you cancel before the trial ends; after that, cancelling ends the subscription at the end of the current billing period. Only the free Basic account works without a card.

    Do I pay VAT on top of the euro price?

    It depends on where you are. The FAQ (read 17 November 2026) says customers in Germany pay VAT, customers elsewhere in the EU pay none if they give a correct VAT ID, and customers outside the EU pay none. Payment is by Visa, American Express, Mastercard or PayPal, and invoices arrive by email as PDF.

    How often can the free account recrawl my site?

    Once a week. The FAQ (read 17 November 2026) says Basic users wait 7 days after each crawl and must start every crawl by hand, while paid plans can recrawl at any time and schedule automatic crawls.
