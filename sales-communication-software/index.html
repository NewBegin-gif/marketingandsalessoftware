Frequently asked questions

What communication tools do sales teams actually need?

Usually a reliable way to call and text prospects (a business phone or dialer), and a way to keep those conversations tied to your CRM. App-first virtual numbers suit small teams; smart routing matters once you have a real sales desk.

Can AI handle sales calls or follow-ups?

AI is good at the repetitive edges — qualifying, scheduling, first-touch follow-up — freeing your team for the conversations that close. Treat it as leverage for your reps, not a replacement, and always keep an easy handoff to a human.

Is there free sales communication software?

Many offer free trials or starter tiers. Test call quality and CRM integration with your real workflow before committing — that's what trials are for. Verify current limits on the provider's site.
