What Elite Site Optimizer does

SEO platform with rank tracking, site crawling and analysis, competitor and backlink reports, spell-checking and an AI citation report, with a 14-day trial.

Pro adds Zeroth Rank and a second domain

Basic covers one site with 100 keywords. Pro is the first plan with Zeroth Rank and a second domain and user, and Pro+ is for agencies that need five domains and 2,500 keywords. The AI Citation Report is priced separately at $99 a month, and the page does not say what it tracks per run, so ask before assuming it comes with a base plan.

How to actually use Elite Site Optimizer

Start the 14-day free trial on elitesiteoptimizer.com (no card needed), add your domain and a campaign, and enter the keywords you want to track. Run a site analysis to get the crawl report, then check rank and competitor reports. The public documentation is short and we did not run the setup ourselves.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Elite Site Optimizer cost?

Monthly prices read on 5 November 2026: Basic $49, Pro $69, Pro+ $119 and a separate AI Citation Report at $99; Enterprise is custom. Annual totals read on 15 October 2026 were $470, $662, $1,142 and $999. The page shows dollar signs without naming the currency.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: a 14-day trial of the full platform, and the page says no credit card is required. We could not confirm whether there is a permanent free plan.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

The pricing FAQ says there is a 30-day money-back guarantee, but the refund policy and the terms say purchased subscriptions are non-refundable. We could not reconcile the two, so check with the vendor before relying on a refund.

Can I cancel any time?

The terms allow cancellation at any time through support or in the application. Access continues to the end of the paid period and the next billing cycle is not charged; accrued overages stay payable.

What does the AI Citation Report do?

It is sold as a separate card at $99 a month and tracks where a brand appears in answers from AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. The page does not publish quotas or say whether it needs a base plan.
