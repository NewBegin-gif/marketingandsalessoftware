Doba sells four subscription tiers. Billed monthly, Limited is $29.99 a month, Basic $59.99, Standard $149.99 and Enterprise $299.99. Quarterly billing takes 5% off (Limited $28.67, Basic $57.00, Standard $142.67 a month) and annual billing 15% (Limited $25.50, Basic $51.00, Standard $127.50 a month); Enterprise is sold monthly or annually only. Paid add-ons such as the AI Store Builder renew on their own cycle.

What Doba does

Dropshipping marketplace that connects online sellers with wholesale suppliers, with one-click listing to Shopify, eBay, Amazon, TikTok Shop and other channels, inventory lists and AI listing tools.

Limited is a test drive; the catalogue opens at Basic

The tiers differ in how much of the catalogue you can actually move into your stores. Limited at $29.99 a month connects one store, allows 30 one-click listings and an inventory list of 20 products, and its plan card lists no monthly product downloads. Basic at $59.99 is the first tier that looks like a working store: two store integrations, 250 one-click listings, 600 products on the inventory list, 450 product downloads a month and 30 AI auto-listings.

Standard at $149.99 is where Doba puts the tools for sellers who run several stores: five integrations, 1,000 listings, 3,000 inventory places, 2,000 downloads a month, API access at 3 requests a second, a dedicated account manager and FTP access. Enterprise at $299.99 raises the counts to 15 stores and 15,000 inventory places with API access at 5 requests a second. Remember that every tier adds the cost of the products and shipping on each order, so the subscription is the smaller line once you sell.

How to actually use Doba

Register a retailer account with an email address or a social login. A new account starts as a "No Plan" account, which according to the help centre only shows product titles and images, so you choose a plan before you can work with the catalogue: open Pricing Plan, pick the plan and the billing cycle (monthly, quarterly or yearly) and subscribe. To sell through Shopify, go to Stores in the left menu, click Connect a New Store, select Shopify and follow the authorisation page there.

Before you take the $0.99 trial, set a reminder: the full fee of the tier is charged automatically when the trial ends.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Doba cost?

Read 22 October 2026 in USD, on monthly billing: $29.99 a month for Limited, $59.99 for Basic, $149.99 for Standard and $299.99 for Enterprise. Quarterly billing takes 5% off and annual billing 15% (Limited $25.50 a month billed annually); Enterprise is monthly or annual only. Product costs, shipping, handling and transaction charges are paid separately.

Is there a free plan or trial?

There is no free working plan: a new account is a "No Plan" account that only shows product titles and images. Basic has a 7-day and Standard a 5-day trial, each for US$0.99, for first-time users only, and the full fee is charged automatically when the trial ends.

Can I get a refund?

Doba's pages disagree. The help centre says a full refund is issued automatically if you cancel within 72 hours of payment; the fees policy says trial subscriptions are not eligible for refunds; the terms say all fees, including subscription fees, are non-refundable. Ask Doba in writing before you rely on a refund.

How do I cancel?

Under Pricing Plan, choose Cancel Subscription and pick whether to cancel immediately or at the end of the billing cycle. Plans renew automatically, so cancel before the next billing date. The terms also mention liquidated damages if you end a commitment period early, so check the confirmation page when you sign up for a promotion.

Which plan do I need?

Count stores and products. Limited allows one store, 30 one-click listings and 20 products on the inventory list; Basic two stores, 250 listings, 600 inventory places and 450 downloads a month; Standard five stores, 1,000 listings and 3,000 inventory places; Enterprise 15 stores and 15,000 inventory places.

Do I keep access to Doba after I cancel?

No. Doba's fees policy at doba.com/site/fees says that when your subscription is cancelled, your access to the Doba site is terminated as well (citation checked on 25 October 2026). Before you cancel, save anything you still need from your account, such as product lists, order history and supplier details, and check how listings already pushed to your store are handled, because the policy does not describe a read-only period after cancellation.
