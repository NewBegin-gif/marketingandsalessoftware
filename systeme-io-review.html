What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The page does not print the annual total, only that it gives 2 months free. In our test (18 Nov 2026) a new funnel went live on the default subdomain at once, and Free pages carried a "Powered by systeme.io" badge. We have not paid for a plan, so email delivery at scale is unknown to us.

What Systeme.io does

All-in-one platform for online businesses that combines sales funnels, email marketing, online courses, a website builder and an affiliate program, with a free plan for up to 2,000 contacts.

Paid plans raise contact, funnel and course limits

The paid plans raise the limits, not the toolset. Startup at $17 a month lifts the cap to 5,000 contacts, 10 funnels and 5 courses. Webinar at $47 goes to 10,000 contacts, 50 funnels and 20 courses and adds evergreen webinars. Unlimited at $97 removes the caps on contacts, funnels, courses and automation rules. Free is enough for a list under 2,000 contacts with 3 funnels and 1 course, as long as one automation rule and one email campaign are enough.

The step that matters is Startup, because it is the first plan past those limits. Webinar is only worth it if you want evergreen webinars or have more than 5,000 contacts.

    How much does Systeme.io cost?

    Per the pricing page read on 17 November 2026, there are four plans in US dollars: Free at $0, Startup at $17 a month, Webinar at $47 and Unlimited at $97. The page also offers annual billing and says it gives 2 months free, without printing the yearly total.

    Is there a free plan?

    Yes. It never expires and needs no credit card. The tighter limits are the ones that bite first: one automation rule, one email campaign and one course, next to 2,000 contacts and 3 sales funnels. The comparison table read on 18 November 2026 also caps Free at 1 tag, 1 workflow, 1 CRM pipeline, 1 website, 1 blog and 15 funnel steps, with no evergreen webinars, and the page structured data says Free processes up to $10,000 in total sales. The full list of Free limits is in the pricing section above.

    Is a new funnel public straight away?

    In our test (18 Nov 2026), yes. A new funnel was set to Active and could be opened on the default systeme.io subdomain at once, so finish it before you share the address. Pages on Free carried a "Powered by systeme.io" badge.

    What decides which plan I need?

    Mainly contacts. The page says Free covers up to 2,000, Startup up to 5,000 (10 funnels, 5 courses), Webinar up to 10,000 (50 funnels, 20 courses, evergreen webinars) and Unlimited has no cap on contacts, funnels, courses or automation rules.

    Are there transaction fees?

    No. The pricing page lists 0% transaction fees on all plans when you accept payments through Stripe or PayPal. The payment processors charge their own fees.

    Can I send unlimited emails?

    The pricing page says every plan includes unlimited email sends to your contacts.

    Can I cancel or downgrade?

    Yes. You cancel in your account settings and keep the plan until the next billing date, then the account reverts to the free plan. To downgrade you can ask support to convert the remaining days into the lower plan, or cancel and upgrade again once the account is on Free.

    Does it help me move from another tool?

    The pricing page lists free migration as a perk and shows a "Free migration" label next to the plans. We have not tried a migration.

    What do we not know?

    How well the email delivery performs at scale, how the course builder works, and what the annual total is, since the page does not print it. We tried the free plan in our test on 18 November 2026, but we have not paid for a plan.
