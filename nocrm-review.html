noCRM Review (2026)The Team Features Are All on Dream

Sales & CRMResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-22

The pitch is that salespeople hate CRMs, and the answer is a tool built around the next action rather than the form.

Best for: solo sellers, small outbound teams and telesales operations that want a list of leads each carrying a next step, rather than a CRM to configure — and European buyers who care that it is built and hosted in the EU, ISO 27001 certified and billed in euros.
Who should skip it: if you want email to live inside the tool without paying for the top tier, because Starter and Expert are BCC-only; you need WhatsApp, VoIP dialling or call recording, all of which are Dream-only; you are running a team and expected roles, permissions and performance reports below €33 a user a month; or you wanted a free plan rather than a 15-day trial.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 16 Sep 2026

The plans
  • Starter — €11/user/month billed annually, €19/user/month billed monthly
    500 leads in one pipeline, quick lead capture, prospecting lists, duplicate detection, categories and tags, up to 4 custom fields, smart follow-ups, calendar sync, mobile app, customisable call scripts, 5,000 emails a month, BCC-only email. New customers only.
  • Expert — €22/user/month billed annually, €29/user/month billed monthly
    Adds unlimited leads and pipelines, unlimited custom fields, quotes and invoices, post-sales tasks, quick custom actions, 3,000+ Zapier automations, API access at 2,000 requests a day, standard webhooks and limited native integrations. Email is still BCC only.
  • Dream — €33/user/month billed annually, €39/user/month billed monthly
    Adds two-way email sync with templates, shared inboxes and email tracking; WhatsApp lead creation and conversation sync; VoIP integrations with CloudTalk, JustCall and Aircall including power dialling, call recording and transcripts; internal automations; roles and permissions, team goal tracking and performance reports; unlimited native integrations; priority support and personalised onboarding from five users.

Three per-seat tiers and no free plan. The page opens on annual billing, where Starter is €11, Expert €22 and Dream €33 per user a month; switch the toggle to monthly and the same tiers are €19, €29 and €39. That makes the annual saving 42% on Starter, 24% on Expert and 15% on Dream, so the "save up to 40%" badge is carried entirely by the cheapest tier.

Starter is capped at 500 leads in a single pipeline with at most four custom fields, and it is sold to new customers only. Every tier includes 5,000 emails a month and all users on one account must sit on the same plan. There is no free tier: a 15-day trial runs without a credit card, or 30 days if you add one, and the account is paused rather than downgraded when it ends.

Checkout is through Stripe, and 20% VAT is added for French companies and for EU businesses without a valid VAT number. One thing to read twice — in the annual view the Starter card reads €11/user/year while the comparison table directly beneath it reads €11/user/month for the same plan. The month is the correct one: it is what the page's own plan data carries for every other tier and what the monthly figure of €19 is measured against. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

What the plan table shows is that almost everything a sales team needs to work that way sits on the top tier. Two-way email sync is Dream at €33 a user; on Starter and on Expert, email is BCC only, which means you copy noCRM on outgoing mail and read replies elsewhere. WhatsApp lead creation and conversation sync are Dream. The VoIP integrations the homepage leads with — CloudTalk, JustCall, Aircall, power dialling, call recording, transcripts — are Dream.

Roles and permissions, lead assignment, team goal tracking and team performance reports are Dream. Internal automations are Dream. noCRM's own FAQ calls Expert "the best fit for most teams", and Expert has no team management in it at all. So the honest way to read the ladder is that Starter and Expert are versions for one person — a lead list with follow-ups, and then the same thing without the caps — while the product the marketing describes starts at €33 a user a month on annual billing, or €39 monthly.

That is three times the entry price, and it is the number to budget from if more than one person is selling. Two smaller things. Starter is restricted to new customers, so it is not a tier you can fall back to when a year on Expert turns out to be more than you needed. And there is no free plan behind the trial: 15 days, or 30 if you hand over a card first.

What noCRM does

Action-driven sales tool from France's Positive Group: every lead carries a next step instead of a form to fill in. Three per-seat tiers — Starter €11, Expert €22, Dream €33 per user a month on annual billing (€19/€29/€39 monthly) — but two-way email, WhatsApp, VoIP and team permissions are Dream only. 15-day trial, no free plan.

Dream is not the top tier, it is the team tier

Read the ladder by what each rung unlocks and the shape is unusual: the first two rungs buy capacity, and only the third buys capability. Starter at €11 a user on annual billing (€19 monthly) is a capped single-seat tool — 500 leads, one pipeline, four custom fields — with the core loop intact: capture, categorise, follow up, call script, mobile app. Expert at €22 (€29 monthly) removes the caps and adds the commercial layer: unlimited leads and pipelines, unlimited custom fields, quotes and invoices, post-sales tasks, Zapier with 3,000+ automations and an API at 2,000 requests a day.

For one person selling, that is the sensible stop. Dream at €33 (€39 monthly) is where the product the homepage advertises actually starts. Two-way email with templates, shared inboxes and open tracking; WhatsApp; the VoIP stack; internal automations; and the entire team layer — roles and permissions, lead assignment, goal tracking, performance reports, plus priority support and personalised onboarding from five users. There is no way to buy the team half without the communication half or the other way round, and no mixing tiers inside one account: everyone sits on the same plan, so one person needing call recording puts the whole team on €33.

What no tier changes is the shape of the tool. It does not become marketing automation or a support desk at any price — those are separate Positive Group products, and a paying noCRM subscription buys discounts on them (20% off Rapidmail and Signitic yearly Standard plans, 20% off Surfer yearly, an extra 15% on Iconosquare Scale and Excel) rather than access.

How to actually use noCRM

Size the tier on communication before you size it on volume, because the caps are not what will move you up the ladder. Write down whether replies need to land in noCRM, whether anyone dials from it, and whether one person needs to see another's leads. Any yes to those is Dream at €33 a user a month annually, and no amount of Expert gets you there. Start the trial without a card.

It runs 15 days that way and 30 if you add payment details, and when it ends the account is paused rather than deleted, so nothing is lost by taking the shorter one first. Use those days on the part that is hard to undo: import a real list from Excel, CSV or email, set your pipeline steps, and check that the next-action prompt fits how you actually work, because that prompt is the product.

If you are on Starter, watch the 500-lead ceiling and the four custom fields — and know that Starter is sold to new customers only, so it is a starting point rather than a place to retreat to. Whichever tier you pick, note that all users on an account share one plan and that new seats are billed pro rata, while removed licences stay live until the end of the period.

Frequently asked questions

What does noCRM cost?

There are three tiers, all priced per seat, and no free plan behind them. On annual billing that is €11, €22 and €33 per user a month for Starter, Expert and Dream; paying month to month costs €19, €29 and €39 instead. The tier prices are only half the answer, because two rules decide the real bill. Everyone on one account has to sit on the same plan, so a single colleague who needs two-way email sync moves the whole team to €33. And Starter is capped at 500 leads in one pipeline and sold to new customers only, which makes it a way in rather than somewhere to retreat to when budgets tighten.

Which tier do I need if email has to live inside the CRM?

Dream, at €33 a user a month on annual billing. Email synchronisation is BCC only on both Starter and Expert, so on those tiers you copy noCRM on outgoing mail and read the replies elsewhere; two-way sync, templates, shared inboxes and email performance tracking all start at Dream. The same tier carries WhatsApp, the VoIP integrations, internal automations and the whole team layer. And because all users on one account must sit on the same plan, one person needing any of that puts everybody on €33.

Is there a free plan, and what happens when the trial ends?

There is no free plan. Every tier comes with a 15-day free trial that needs no credit card, and adding payment details extends it to 30 days. When it runs out the account is paused rather than downgraded or deleted, so nothing is lost by taking the shorter trial first. Note one restriction before you plan a retreat path: Starter is sold to new customers only, so it is a starting point rather than a tier you can fall back to later.

What should I weigh before buying Sales & CRM software?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.
