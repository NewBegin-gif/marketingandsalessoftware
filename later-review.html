Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

Starter $18.75/month, Growth $37.50 and Scale $82.50, all billed yearly, which Later advertises as three months free and 25% off against monthly. There is a limited free plan and a 14-day trial. The add-ons are where a growing team pays more: extra Social Sets are $11.25 a month each, extra users $3.75 a month each, and extra AI credits $3.75 a month per 100 credits.

Starter covers casual scheduling; Growth adds the inbox, approvals and longer analytics; Scale is aimed at enterprise-level analytics without an enterprise contract. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

Read the published ladder as the price of ONE social set, because the add-ons are where a growing account actually pays. An extra Social Set is $11.25 a month, which is sixty per cent of the entire Starter plan. Two extra sets cost more than the plan itself. Extra users are $3.75 each and extra AI credits $3.75 per hundred. So an agency or a creator running three brands is not paying $18.75; they are paying $41.25 before adding a single teammate, which is more than the Growth plan that would have come with more capability.

Count your social sets first and the tier second. Within Starter the caps are the real ceiling: 30 posts per profile and three months of analytics. For an active brand posting daily that is a month's work and a quarter of history, so the honest comparison is at Growth, which adds the inbox, approvals and longer analytics. Scale exists for enterprise-level analytics without an enterprise contract. There is a limited free plan and a 14-day trial.

What to judge in it is not the scheduling — everything in this category schedules — but whether the visual-first planning matches how you actually work. That grid-and-preview approach is Later's inheritance from Instagram and remains its real strength; if your world is link-in-bio commerce and visual planning, the focus earns its seat, and if you need deep multi-network publishing and listening, a suite-class tool fits better at any price.

How to actually use Later

Compare at the tier you will actually need, because Starter's caps are where the real price lives. Thirty posts per profile is about one a day, and three months of analytics cannot show you a year-over-year comparison or a seasonal pattern. An active brand runs out of both quickly, which puts it on Growth at $37.50 rather than Starter at $18.75. Count your Social Sets before anything else, since extra ones are $11.25 a month each — sixty per cent of Starter's entire price for one more brand.

An agency with three clients is buying three sets, and that number will dominate the comparison against any per-seat competitor. Extra users are $3.75 a month and extra AI credits $3.75 per hundred. Note which prices you are reading: the published figures are the yearly rates, which Later advertises as three months free and 25% off monthly. Use the 14-day trial or the limited free plan to test the thing Later is actually best at: visual planning of a grid.

If your world is link-in-bio commerce and how the feed looks as a whole, that focus is what you are paying for. If it is not, a general scheduler will serve you as well for less.

Frequently asked questions

What does Later cost?

Billed yearly, $18.75 a month for Starter, $37.50 for Growth and $82.50 for Scale, which Later presents as 25% off and three months free against monthly billing. Read on 17 August 2026.

What pushes the bill above the plan price?

Three add-ons, all priced per month: an extra Social Set is $11.25, an extra user $3.75, and 100 extra AI credits $3.75. A team of four managing two brands is therefore paying the plan plus roughly $22 before anything else, so count sets and seats before comparing tiers.

Which tier do I need?

Starter is single-user casual scheduling. Growth is the first tier with the engagement inbox and approval workflows, which is what agencies and teams actually need. Scale is about analytics depth rather than more posting. If more than one person touches the calendar, Starter will not hold.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
