What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Unlocking an email address costs 2 credits, a phone number 5, intent data 3, and adding a lead to a list costs another credit. Growth's 2,000 credits are therefore about 660 leads a month with a verified email, or about 250 if you also want the phone number. Sending is billed the same way on a separate balance: a plain email costs 1 email credit, but an AI-personalised email written by SalesTarget's own AI costs 5, so Growth's 5,000 emails become 1,000 if every step is AI-written.

Connecting your own LLM key brings that down to 2. None of this is hidden, but none of it is on the pricing page either. The 'Super Saver' badge on the bundle is hard to check. Lead Explorer Launch ($49, 2,000 credits) plus Email Outreach Growth ($30, 5,000 emails) comes to $79 for the same allowances as the $169 All In One Growth plan. The $90 difference buys one LinkedIn automation slot and Copilot, and the LinkedIn tab showed no stand-alone price when we read it on 1 October 2026, so we cannot tell you whether the bundle actually saves money.

The yearly toggle has the same problem: it says 5%, and the real discount runs from 0% to about 15% depending on the module. Two documents disagree on how to leave. The pricing FAQ says you can cancel any time from the dashboard with no cancellation fee, while the terms say subscriptions auto-renew unless 30 days' notice is given and that partial terms are not refunded. Cancel well before the renewal date and keep a record.

The 'Labor Day Sale, Limited Time Only' banner still running on 1 October is a small thing, but it is the first thing on every page. The LinkedIn side is cautious by design: the help centre starts a standard LinkedIn account at 3 connection requests a day, takes 42 days to reach 20 a day, caps it at 100 a week, and halves your daily limit if fewer than 30% of requests are accepted.

We found no independent reviews we could read: there is no Product Hunt listing, and G2 and Trustpilot blocked our reader. We have not used the product.

What SalesTarget.ai does

All-in-one B2B outbound platform: a lead database of 840M+ professional profiles with credit-based enrichment, cold email with unlimited inboxes and warm-up, LinkedIn automation, and a built-in CRM and dialer, sold as separate modules or one bundle per team.

Buy the module you use, not the bundle

If you only send cold email to lists you already have, Email Outreach on its own is the cheapest way in: $30 a month for 5,000 emails, or $85 for 100,000, with unlimited inboxes and warm-up included. If you only need data, Lead Explorer starts at $49 for 2,000 credits. Both together cost $79, less than half the $169 All In One Growth plan. The bundle only makes sense if you will actually run LinkedIn campaigns, since the LinkedIn slot and Copilot are what the extra $90 pays for.

Scale at $319 is the step for a team that sends more than 5,000 emails a month: it includes 100,000 emails and 4,000 credits, while Lead Explorer Builder ($99) and Email Outreach Hypergrowth ($85) together cost $184, which leaves $135 for two LinkedIn slots. Ultimate at $679 is for very high volume senders. Pay monthly until you know your volume; the yearly discount is small, and nothing on Lead Explorer's lower tiers.

How to actually use SalesTarget.ai

Start with the free trial, which needs no card and gives you 50 credits. Searching Lead Explorer is free, so build and filter a list first, then turn on the 'Has Email' toggle so you only spend credits on leads whose email can actually be unlocked. The help centre says you are not charged when no verified result is found, and leads you already unlocked are never charged again. Before you send anything, decide how much AI personalisation you need.

Use plain templates for follow-ups and AI only on the first step, or connect your own LLM key under Settings, Preferences, User LLM, which brings the cost per AI email from 5 credits to 2. If you connect a LinkedIn account, leave warm-up mode on and do not raise the daily limits, because a restricted account pauses every campaign using it. On a paid plan, check the amount at checkout: on the yearly toggle the buttons still read '/mo' next to the yearly total.

Note your renewal date when you subscribe; the terms ask for 30 days' notice before renewal, even though the FAQ says you can cancel any time.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SalesTarget.ai cost?

The All In One bundle is $169, $319 or $679 a month; Lead Explorer alone starts at $49 for 2,000 credits and Email Outreach alone at $30 for 5,000 emails. Plans are per organisation with 3 seats included. Read on salestarget.ai/pricing on 1 October 2026.

How far do SalesTarget credits go?

An email address costs 2 credits, a phone number 5 and adding a lead to a list 1, so 2,000 credits are about 660 leads with an email. Sending uses separate email credits: 1 per plain email, 5 per email written by SalesTarget's AI.

Can I cancel SalesTarget.ai any time?

The FAQ says yes, from the dashboard, without a refund for the current period. The terms say subscriptions auto-renew unless you give 30 days' notice, so cancel well before the renewal date.
