Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 13 Oct 2026

One plan, called Complete, at $25 a month, billed in USD every 30 days, with a 14-day free trial and no free tier. There is no response limit and no per-response charge: unlimited post-purchase, NPS, CSAT and attribution responses are included at that price, on every Shopify plan. The single price also covers revenue and AOV reporting per marketing channel, targeting by order value, product, country, payment method and customer, delivery on-site, by email and on Shopify POS, exports to Shopify Flow, Klaviyo and GA4, and querying your survey data with your own AI assistant through the vendor's MCP server. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

There is no free tier and no small plan, so from day fifteen the app costs $25 a month whether it collects four responses or forty thousand: a low-volume store pays the same as a high-volume one, and several of the nearest alternatives do publish a free plan. The second thing to weigh is access rather than price: the listing's data section asks for customer data including sensitive, device and activity data, plus orders, staff accounts and the online store, which is a lot of reach for a survey tool and worth reading the privacy policy over.

The evidence base is otherwise good for a Shopify app: launched 17 August 2017 by Union Works Apps in London, and 232 reviews on 13 October 2026 of which 225 are five-star and none below four.

There is nothing to upgrade to

One plan at $25 a month, nothing above it and nothing below it, so the only decision left is Grapevine against the meter its rivals run. Its own pricing page makes that argument openly: unlimited surveys and unlimited responses, every integration from the moment you install, the same $25 whether you collect fifty responses a month or fifty thousand, and no inflated monthly rate designed to push you onto an annual contract.

Do that comparison at your own volume, because a flat fee is the expensive option at the bottom of the range. A store collecting a handful of answers a month can sit on a competitor's free tier for nothing while this costs $300 a year; the flat rate only starts winning at the volume where a usage-priced app would have moved you up a tier. What the $25 does not buy is a response rate.

Placement, display rules and how many questions you ask decide that, and since none of them sit behind a paywall here, a survey nobody answers cannot be fixed by spending more. It does not buy data custody either: uninstall, and everything is deleted after thirty days.

    How to actually use Grapevine

    Let the setup assistant build the first survey. There are three routes in (the assistant, the template library, or from scratch) and the assistant is the short one. Decide where the survey runs before you decide what it asks. The options are the thank-you page, the order status page, a hosted on-site page with its own URL, an embed on any page, one-click links in email, and a Shopify POS tile.

    On the two post-purchase pages the vendor's own advice is to place the app block near the top; further down the page costs you answers. Use Display Rules rather than a second survey to split audiences: new against returning, order value, products in the order, or the customer tags you already keep in Shopify, so first-time buyers and VIPs get different questions without rebuilding those segments inside the app. Switch on the one-response-per-customer setting before you launch, not after.

    And export before you leave: the CSV sits in each survey's Report or Responses view, and uninstalling wipes responses, stats and configurations permanently after thirty days.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Grapevine?

    Exit check 2026-11-17; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    Will my survey be translated automatically for customers in other languages?

    No. The help article 'Can my survey automatically be translated into another language?' (read 13 October 2026) says surveys are not translated by location or language. You create one post-purchase survey per checkout language and write every question, answer option and thank-you message yourself, then assign the language under Manage Visibility; for email the suggestion is one on-site survey page per language segment, and on Shopify POS a different survey per location. For a store selling in several languages that means a survey per language to build and keep in step.

    What customer data does Grapevine keep, and can I sign a data processing agreement?

    Per 'What do you do with the data that you collect?' (5 June 2026) and 'GDPR and our Data Processing Agreement' (29 December 2025), read 13 October 2026: the app requests Shopify customer and order data at install but stores only survey questions, answers and responses, the data needed to report revenue against responses, and the customer's name and email in the response listing; the rest is ignored. Union Works Ltd (UK) acts as data processor for your customers' data, has appointed an external data protection officer, and offers a DPA you can sign online. That narrows what is kept, not the access scopes the Shopify listing requests.
