Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

The only published price is Startup at $600/mo on an annual term, covering 2 users, with additional users available to purchase; Growth and Elite are listed as Custom, so those numbers come from sales rather than the site. All plans include a free trial. Startup covers 13,500 email address credits and 600 phone number credits per user per year, 2 mailboxes per user and 27,000 contacts.

Growth lists 4 users, 35,000 email and 5,000 phone credits per user per year, 4 mailboxes and 140,000 contacts. Elite lists 10 users, 40,000 email and 9,600 phone credits, 8 mailboxes and 400,000 contacts. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The independent picture is genuinely strong overall: around 4.6/5 across ~600 G2 reviews, with repeated praise for unusually human-sounding AI messages, ease of use and hands-on support. But data accuracy and email deliverability are the most-cited complaints, and they cluster on European contacts — North American data holds up better than EMEA/APAC. Amplemarket points to its own benchmarks showing competitive accuracy, so weigh both sides and test their data against your own target region in a trial before you commit.

Under ~10 reps you'll also pay for features you never touch. The genuinely new bit: it now works inside ChatGPT and Claude. Since June that integration can triage the inbox and update or remove leads rather than just search, so reps can run more of the workflow without leaving the chat. Since July that stretches further. From the same chat a rep can research an account, pull enriched profiles and enrol every contact into one sequence with copy written per person and per step.

A GTM Skills Library exposes the rest of the platform the same way. Amplemarket says setup takes under two minutes — that is their figure, not one we timed.

What Amplemarket does

An all-in-one AI sales platform: an AI 'Searcher' that turns a plain-English ICP into verified contacts, 100+ contact-level buying-intent signals, and social prospecting from LinkedIn, plus multichannel outreach and deliverability tooling — drawing on a large multi-source contact database it refreshes continuously. Its Duo copilot proactively surfaces prioritized leads from 20+ real-time signal types, and an MCP server puts the platform inside ChatGPT or Claude — where it can triage the Unibox, update or remove leads, and since July run a whole account-based play end to end: research the account, pull enriched profiles, and enrol every contact into one sequence with copy written per person and per step, without leaving the chat. A GTM Skills Library exposes the rest of the platform the same way. Duo Voice is the audio arm of the same copilot: you read one short sample aloud, it clones your voice, and a voice-message step can then be dropped into any sequence, with the message written per prospect from their data and adapted to their language — LinkedIn voice notes rather than another block of text. Since June you can also describe a workflow in plain English and have it built for you in the normal editor, and meeting notes from Fathom and Attention flow into the activity feed. Deliverability is a named toolkit rather than a footnote: a Domain Health Center for SPF/DKIM/DMARC, inbox-to-outbox ratio, spam-list and mailbox-age monitoring; automated warm-up and recovery for flagged Gmail or Outlook mailboxes; weekly spam-placement tests before a campaign goes out; and Duo rotating the sending mailbox per email against that same health data. Workflows, the automation layer, is included for every customer rather than sold as a premium add-on: triggers (a sequence finishing with no reply, a bounce, a meeting booked, a call logged, a CRM field changing), filters for who qualifies, branching paths and actions (enrol in another sequence, update CRM fields, create tasks, manage lists, enrich, add to exclusion lists), assembled on a visual canvas so the whole logic is readable at a glance. Twenty ready-made Recipes cover lead routing, keeping non-responders warm, account plays, CRM hygiene and do-not-contact enforcement. It runs against Salesforce and HubSpot out of the box, and without a CRM connected the triggers and actions that don't depend on one still work. The browser extension carries the same work into whatever page you are on: adding and editing notes, creating and running tasks, and filtering the activity feed without switching tabs.

What buyers report about Amplemarket

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Amplemarket ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The AI-written messages read more human than the competition — reviewers name that specifically, and it pulls from LinkedIn, company news and job postings to do it. 4.6 on G2 across 598 reviews, 76% five-star and no one- or two-star ratings at all.
  • • Customer service rates 5.0 on Capterra, which is close to unheard of.
  • • TrustRadius reviewers from October 2025 describe consolidating several data and sequencing subscriptions into this one, and singling out decision-makers is the job they say it does best.
What they complain about
  • • From $600 a month, annual only. The minimum is $7,200 a year for two users on limited credits, and reviewers report real costs landing 40-60% higher once Sales Navigator, extra seats and credit overages are in.
  • • Bounce rates of 20-30% are reported on exported lists, which is enough to damage your sending domain. Verify before you send.
  • • Data accuracy drops noticeably outside the US.
  • • Missing features is the top G2 complaint tag, and 32 reviewers name a steep learning curve — buying-signal configuration in particular.
  • • Data quality is named as a weakness on TrustRadius too, by reviewers who otherwise rate it highly — a second platform reaching the same conclusion as G2 and Capterra rather than an isolated complaint.
  • • Also named there: ICP and company matching that sometimes picks the wrong targets, automated sequences that underperform expectations, intent data judged not worthwhile, no auto top-up for credits, and gaps in CRM integration coverage.

Where the ratings disagree. Read TrustRadius knowing that essentially all of its recent Amplemarket reviews carry an 'Incentivized' label, so the tone runs warm. The value is that even in that warm sample the data-accuracy complaint still surfaces.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is no free tier. The only published price is Startup: $600/mo on an annual term, covering 2 users, 13,500 email address credits and 600 phone number credits per user per year, 2 mailboxes per user and 27,000 contacts. Growth and Elite are listed as "Custom", so those numbers come from sales, not the site. What you buy by moving up is volume, not a different product. Growth lists 4 users, 35,000 email and 5,000 phone credits per user per year, 4 mailboxes per user and 140,000 contacts.

Elite lists 10 users, 40,000 email and 9,600 phone credits, 8 mailboxes per user and 400,000 contacts, and is where Duo Inbox is included rather than sold as an add-on. The upgrade pays back if calling and multi-mailbox sending are your constraint — phone credits going from 600 to 5,000 is the biggest proportional jump in the table. It does not pay back if your problem is data accuracy in a given region, and it does not shorten the annual commitment.

Note that Amplemarket's own pricing post states different Startup allowances (15,000 email, 480 phone credits) than the pricing page, and prices phone top-ups at $0.50 each: get your allowance written into the contract.

How to actually use Amplemarket

The vendor's own route to first value is short. Amplemarket University's Getting Started course is 6 videos and about 10 minutes total: connect a mailbox, install the Chrome extension, link LinkedIn, then Searcher, save a search, build a sequence from that saved search, launch it, and watch the sequence overview and tasks page. Their onboarding page says customers can "start producing real results in days" — their claim, not our timing.

Distilled from vendor material only: Amplemarket University (amplemarket.com/university), the knowledge base collection on domains and mailboxes (knowledge.amplemarket.com) and Amplemarket's onboarding page — we found no independent hands-on walkthrough worth citing; the third-party pages that surfaced were competitor blogs or affiliate comparison pages, not independent reviews.

Common Amplemarket setup mistakes

Where Amplemarket setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

Where teams stall is the layer underneath.

  • The knowledge base collection for domains and mailboxes is dominated by SPF/DKIM/DMARC recipes, one per registrar-and-mail-host pair (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, Squarespace, Amazon, DNSimple; Google Workspace or Microsoft 365).
  • Amplemarket recommends a minimum of 2 domains for a sales team, and Startup includes 2 mailboxes per user, so DNS work and warm-up time set your real start date, not the software.
  • Some knowledge-base articles, safety settings among them, sit behind a customer login, so you cannot read those limits before signing.

Frequently asked questions

What does Amplemarket cost?

$600 a month on an annual term for the Startup plan, which includes two users; Growth and Elite are quote-only. That is the only figure Amplemarket publishes, and it has been unchanged across our July and August 2026 captures and our re-read on 17 August 2026. The floor is therefore $7,200 a year for two seats, and reviewers report real invoices landing well above that once credits and extra users are added.

How does it compare to ZoomInfo on what you get?

That comparison is one of the measured searches that brings people here, and the honest answer is that the products are shaped differently: Amplemarket bundles the data with the sending, so credits and mailboxes are part of the plan (13,500 email and 600 phone credits per user per year on Startup, 2 mailboxes per user). Buyers on G2, Capterra and TrustRadius consistently praise the message quality and consistently flag data accuracy outside the US, which is the trade to test on your own territory during the trial.

Is there a way to try it before committing to a year?

All plans include a free trial, per their pricing page on 17 August 2026, but the published plan itself is annual — there is no published monthly option. Use the trial to check the two things reviewers flag most: bounce rates on an exported list before you send to it, and data quality in your actual target geography.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
