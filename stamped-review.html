When the paid tier is worth it

Stamped bills on monthly order volume rather than on a flat plan fee, and the single most useful thing we found is that the bundle undercuts the single product. We read their pricing page on 11 August 2026 with the bundle toggle on and all three products ticked.

The three-product bundle came to about $18 a month at the 200-order step, while Reviews on its own was about $23 at the same step. At 1,000 orders it is $79 bundled against $99 for Reviews alone. In other words, at the volumes most stores actually run, buying all three products costs less than buying one.

So if you were planning to start with Reviews and add the others later, check the bundle price before you do — later may cost more than now. The second finding is where the ladder turns against you. How we would start

Install the app from the Shopify app store, then import first: existing reviews go in as a CSV through Settings, Importer. Then set store name, store email, the From address reviews are sent from, and the deliverability settings beside them.

Display is a separate job. On Shopify 2.0 the main widget and star-rating badge are added from the theme customiser as a section and a block; on vintage themes you paste Stamped's liquid snippets into product.liquid or main-product.liquid, and into product-card.liquid for collection pages.

The tracking script goes into theme.liquid before the closing body tag, with your own public API key and store hash. The step teams underestimate is the theme code. Review requests only generate once the Enable Review Requests toggle is saved; installing the app starts nothing.

Stamped documents that any change or update of your theme may remove its widget codes, so a redesign can silently end review display. On non-product pages the main widget loses its Write a Review functionality. Support for this installation is limited and, in Stamped's wording, offered to paid users only.

Who it is for

Best for mid-market Shopify brands that want reviews and loyalty in one platform instead of juggling separate apps.

Skip it if you're an early-stage store — the removed free plan and $199+/product pricing are steep before you have the volume to justify them.

What to weigh it against

The comparison that matters is Yotpo or Judge.me — other reviews and loyalty apps, some far cheaper.

Questions buyers actually ask

Is Stamped really around $800 a month?

Only at volume, and the number gets quoted badly — including by us until we re-read it on 11 August 2026. $798/month is the figure at the 5,000-order step. Is there still a free plan?

No. The free tier is gone and each product — Reviews, Loyalty and Lifecycle — is its own subscription, so an early-stage store is choosing which one to start with rather than growing into the suite. What does a second storefront cost?

$100 a month per additional domain. Their pricing page states that plans include up to two domains and that each further store gets a first month free before that charge starts — wording that has been on the page unchanged in every capture we hold since 7 July 2026. Worth pricing in early if you run regional storefronts, because it lands on top of the per-product subscriptions rather than inside them.
