What you pay once, and what you pay after

There is no recurring fee on the theme itself: these are premium Shopify themes at a one-time price, read on 7 September 2026 and re-read on 12 September 2026. Pixel Union's own six run $100 (Startup) and $120 (Editions) through $280 (Grid) and $300 (Atlantic) to $360 (Empire, Tailor). Sister brand Out of the Sandbox spans $220 to $499, with Turbo at $425 and Flex at $495. What the sticker hides is not the amount but the subscription: the group does sell one, an optional $96-a-year Priority Support plan that renews automatically.

The amount itself is on the theme page, printed beside the 'Try theme for free' button -- though it was not there on 7 September, when the same pages showed the button alone and the price lived only in the store's product data. That shape differs from everything else in this category: a $360 theme costs $360 in year one and nothing in year five. Against a page-builder subscription at even $30 a month it pays for itself inside the first year -- so run that comparison over three years, not one.

What the price does not buy is a finished store, and that is where the money actually goes. A premium theme is a design system and a fast start; the lift comes from how you configure, populate and optimise it. A $360 theme on a store with weak product pages and slow images converts weakly, and the hours spent fixing those are the real budget line. Two ongoing costs are easy to miss on a one-time purchase: a heavily customised theme is more work to keep updated, because each vendor update has to be reconciled with your changes, and switching on every feature adds page weight -- the one thing that reliably costs you conversions on mobile. Enable what you need and leave the rest off.

How to actually use Pixel Union

Buy the theme for the design system and the speed to launch, not as a conversion switch. A $360 theme on a store with weak product photography, thin descriptions and slow images still converts weakly -- the lift comes from how you configure, populate and optimise it, and that work happens after the purchase rather than instead of it. So plan that work before you buy: real photography, real copy, and a page-speed check once the theme is populated with your actual catalogue rather than demo content.

Heavily customised premium themes can add page weight if you switch on every feature, so turn features on deliberately and measure after each one. Establish how updates work before you customise heavily. A theme you have edited extensively is more work to keep current, and that cost arrives months later when you have forgotten what you changed -- keep a written note of every customisation as you make it. Frequently asked questions

Do I pay again for Pixel Union theme updates?

No. The theme pages (read 27 September 2026) list free theme updates and free customer support with every Pixel Union theme, so the one-time price, $100 for Startup up to $360 for Empire or Tailor, is the whole theme cost. The optional $96-a-year Priority Support plan, read on 7 September, is the only recurring charge in the group. What updates do cost is time if you have edited the theme's code, since each update has to be reconciled with your changes.

Does a theme preset cost extra?

No. The store's product data (read 27 September 2026) prices every preset at the theme's own price: Empire, Circuit, Depot and Forma are all $360, and Startup's four presets are all $100. A preset is a starting style, not a separate product, so choose the theme for its structure and the preset for its look.

Can I try a Pixel Union theme before paying?

Yes. Each theme page (read 27 September 2026) carries a 'Try theme for free' button next to the price, alongside several demo presets to compare. Do that trial with your real catalogue and images rather than the demo content, since page weight and layout fit are what decide whether it works for you.

Is Pixel Union one theme or a set of separate products?

Separate products. The pixelunion.net homepage (read 1 November 2026) lists individual Shopify themes, among them Empire, Atlantic, Tailor, Editions and Startup, plus the Out of the Sandbox themes and several apps, each as its own item. Each theme carries its own one-time price, as listed on this page. The homepage shows no bundle, so do not assume one licence covers more than the theme you buy; a multi-theme deal is not confirmed.
