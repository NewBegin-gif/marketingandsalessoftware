1. Keap — Solo & small teams that want CRM + automation in one

CRM, sales and marketing automation built for small businesses. Capture leads, automate follow-up and manage your pipeline in one place.

My take: More than a contact list: Keap bundles the pipeline with email/SMS follow-up and simple automations, so leads don't fall through the cracks while you're busy. Read our full Keap review →

Visit Keap →

2. folk — Founders who hate bloated CRMs

Lightweight, collaborative CRM for managing contacts and relationships. Centralize your network and personalize outreach without the bloat. A mobile app (Contacts by folk) adds on-the-go search, one-tap contact capture with business-card scanning, voice notes and interaction tracking across WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email and calendar.

My take: Lightweight and genuinely pleasant to use — it pulls in your contacts and lets you personalise outreach without a week of setup. Great first CRM. Read our full folk review →

Visit folk →

3. Close — Outbound sales teams living on calls & email

Sales CRM unifying calling, email and SMS in one inbox, for teams that live on the phone. Seats run about $9 (Solo) to $139 (Scale) per user/month billed annually, and call, SMS and Power Dialer credits bill separately on top — a $99 plan can land nearer $160 once a rep dials all day.

My take: Built for high-velocity outreach: calling, emailing and sequences in one window, so reps spend time selling instead of logging activity. Read our full Close review →
