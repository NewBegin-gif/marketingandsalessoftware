Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 28 Sep 2026

Four plans, billed annually: Build $24/month, Optimize $119/month, Scale from $229/month and Enterprise from $1,199/month. Annual billing includes two months free against monthly. Custom domains are metered: 1 on Build, 3 on Optimize, 10 on Scale and 100 on Enterprise, with extras at $5 a month each. AI credits come with the plan (2,500, 10,000, 20,000 and 60,000 a month) and top-ups are pay-as-you-go at $15 for 5,000, $25 for 10,000 and $40 for 20,000.

A promotional rate roughly half the Build price appears periodically; it is a limited-time offer rather than the standard rate. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is no free plan. Landingi publishes a 14-day trial capped at 200 unique visits, and its help centre warns the trial account "may be blocked" once you reach that, so a live campaign forces you onto a paid tier straight away. Build, at $24 per month on annual billing, covers 10 active pages, 2,000 monthly visits and 1 custom domain. That pays for itself if you run a small number of offers with modest traffic and only need to publish, not test.

It stops being enough the moment you want optimisation: A/B testing, EventTracker and Smart Sections sit on Optimize at $119 per month, a large step up for features many buyers assume are the point of a landing page tool. Optimize also lifts you to 100 pages, 30,000 visits and 3 domains. Upgrading does not change how traffic is counted. Landingi counts a visit using a cookie valid for 30 minutes, so the same person returning after that window counts as a new visit against your allowance.

Nor does it make anything portable: the vendor states pages stay live only while your subscription is active.

    How to actually use Landingi

    The fastest honest route is to start the 14-day trial, pick a template from the 400+ library rather than building from a blank canvas, edit it in the visual builder, and publish. Landingi's walkthrough says tooltips lead you through the tabs and features when you log in. The real friction is the domain. Publishing to your own subdomain means adding an A record pointing to 157.53.227.1 at your registrar, then waiting for Landingi to verify it.

    The help centre says a domain is usually active a few hours after adding, and that the wait depends on how many domains are queued for certificate installation. An hourglass icon means it is still verifying; a green globe means it is live. Landingi's docs also warn against pointing www at Landingi when you already have a website on that root domain, because it replaces the existing content. The other trap is the trial's 200-visit cap: hit it and the account may be blocked and the page stops displaying, so do not point a real campaign at a trial page.
