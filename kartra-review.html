When the paid plan is worth it

There is one line in this pricing that changes everything and it is missing from most summaries: Essentials charges a 5% transaction fee on your sales, and every tier above it charges nothing. The step from Essentials to Starter is $60 a month ($59 against $119). At 5%, that $60 is covered by $1,200 of monthly sales, so above roughly $1,200 a month in revenue, the cheapest plan is the most expensive one.

At $5,000 a month Essentials costs $309 against Starter's $119; at $10,000 it is $559 against $119. Below that crossover Essentials is the cheaper plan, which makes it a tier for people who are building rather than selling. What upgrading does not fix is the contact ladder underneath it. The four tiers hold 500, 2,500, 12,500 and 25,000 contacts, and Kartra never refuses a new lead: it moves you to the tier that fits and charges a mid-cycle reconciliation fee for the move.

So the bill can rise without you choosing it, on a page whose own FAQ says there are never any extra fees.

    How to actually use Kartra

    Work out your monthly sales before you choose a tier, because Essentials carries a 5% transaction fee and every tier above it charges nothing. The $60 monthly gap between Essentials and Starter equals 5% of $1,200, so the moment you sell more than $1,200 a month, Starter is literally the cheaper plan. On yearly billing the crossover falls to about $940. Then check the caps while you are there. Essentials sends 10,000 emails and texts a month against unlimited above it and holds 500 contacts; Starter holds 2,500, Growth 12,500 and Professional 25,000.

    Passing the contact cap does not stop your campaigns (Kartra upgrades you automatically and bills a mid-cycle reconciliation fee) so work out which tier your list will land in before it lands there. Use the 14-day trial to build one real funnel end to end: page, form, email sequence, checkout. Kartra's argument is breadth rather than depth, and a fortnight is enough to find out whether the whole loop works for your product.

    What does Kartra cost?

    Essentials $59 a month or $52 billed yearly, Starter $119 / $99, Growth $229 / $189 and Professional $549 / $429, with yearly saving up to 22% and a 14-day trial. Read on 5 September 2026 and unchanged on 30 September 2026, when the page also advertised a 30-day money-back guarantee.

    Why is Essentials cheaper than it looks expensive?

    Because of the transaction fee. Essentials takes 5% of your sales; every tier above it takes 0%. If you sell $2,000 a month that fee is $100, already more than the $60 difference to Starter. Unless you are pre-revenue, the entry tier is usually the more expensive choice: work it out against your own monthly sales before picking it.

    How does it compare with Kajabi?

    That comparison is the single most-searched thing about Kartra on our pages. Both bundle courses, checkout, email and pages into one login, so the honest split is not features but what you give up: Kartra's entry tier is cheaper on paper but carries the 5% sales fee, and its email allowance is capped at 10,000 sends a month until you move up from Essentials. Compare the tier you would actually run on, including the fee, rather than the advertised starting prices.

    Can I still run webinars in Kartra?

    Not for long. Kartra's help centre says that from 1 November 2026 you can no longer create or edit webinars, and Kartra told its affiliates the feature is removed entirely on 1 December 2026. If webinars are part of your funnel, plan for a separate webinar tool. Read on 28 September 2026.

    What can Kartra's built-in AI write for me?
