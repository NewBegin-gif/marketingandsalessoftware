What buyers report about AgencyAnalytics

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run AgencyAnalytics ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Time saved on client reporting — automated reports are the single most-cited reason agencies keep it.
  • • White-label delivery quality, and that the custom domain is on every plan including the cheapest.
  • • Support response speed, rated above 9/10 by G2 reviewers.
What they complain about
  • • No global template management. You cannot push one change across all client reports at once — reviewers have flagged this for two years and it is still open.
  • • Integrations disconnect and need re-authorising.
  • • Several reviewers say the higher plans feel like worse value than the lower ones, so there is little pull to upgrade.

When the paid plan is worth it

AgencyAnalytics publishes one rate: $20 USD per client / month, billed annually, with "Save 20% annually" shown beside it — so paying month to month costs more, and the page does not print that figure. There is no free plan; the only free route in is a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee. The economics are simple: you pay per client, and staff and client users are unlimited, so cost tracks the roster you already invoice and putting a junior on reporting adds nothing.

It pays for itself when you rebuild the same reports for a stable roster every month. It works against you when your client count swings, because you pay per client whether or not that client got a report, and because the discounted rate carries a 1-year commitment while monthly plans have none. Budget the add-ons separately: Rank Tracker at $41.67 USD /month per 500 keywords and AI Tracker at $20.83 USD /month per 250 credits, both billed annually.

Paying more fixes neither recurring complaint: integration outages are, in the vendor's words, "by far the most common reason for missing data", and same-name metrics that are calculated differently stay different at any price. Past 25 clients the vendor asks you to contact them for a custom plan rather than publishing a rate.

How to actually use AgencyAnalytics

The vendor's getting-started guide is eight videos. The first four are Welcome & Quick Start (4 minutes), Quick Account Tour (4.5), Add & Organize Clients (2) and Connect a Data Source (2.5). The rest are Create a Dashboard (7.5), Build and Schedule a Report (4.5), Set Up Branding / White Labeling (3), and Manage Users and Permissions (4). The vendor's own tutorial weighting suggests where the work sits: connecting a source gets 2.5 minutes, while building a dashboard gets 7.5 and scheduling a report another 4.5.

Read that as the vendor's emphasis, not as a measure of your time. A Client is "one business relationship you manage in AgencyAnalytics", and you connect its sources from the 85+ integrations. Plan for numbers that disagree with the native platform. The troubleshooting doc tells you to match the exact start and end dates used in the source platform, and warns that similarly named metrics are calculated differently — its "Clicks" pulls from the "Total Clicks" metric in Facebook Ads.

Integration outages are the vendor's stated most common cause of missing data, so expect reconnection work; the Connect Your Data help collection alone runs to 110 articles.

Frequently asked questions

What do agencies actually say about AgencyAnalytics on Reddit?

"agency analytics reddit" is the single biggest search that reaches this page, so it deserves a straight answer: we do not summarise Reddit threads here, because we cannot verify who wrote them or when. What we can point at is the vendor conceding the most common complaint in its own documentation. AgencyAnalytics' help centre calls integration outages "by far the most common reason for missing data", and its troubleshooting doc warns that similarly named metrics are calculated differently between platforms — its "Clicks" pulls the "Total Clicks" metric from Facebook Ads. If connector reliability is what you are trying to research, that is the vendor answering in writing.

What does AgencyAnalytics cost for a real client roster?

One published rate: $20 USD per client per month, billed annually, with unlimited staff and client users (read on the vendor pricing page 11 August 2026). Twenty-four clients is $480 a month at that rate; from 25 clients up the vendor stops publishing a price and asks you to contact sales for a custom plan. Budget the add-ons separately — Rank Tracker $41.67 a month per 500 keywords, AI Tracker $20.83 a month per 250 credits, both billed annually.

Is there a month-to-month price?

The page advertises "Save 20% annually" but prints no month-to-month figure, so you cannot compare the two from the page itself. What it does say is what you are trading: monthly plans carry no commitment and can be cancelled any time, while the discounted annual plan requires a one-year commitment. The 14-day trial needs no credit card, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee (all read 11 August 2026).

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
