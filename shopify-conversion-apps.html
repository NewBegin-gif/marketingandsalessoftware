Shopify conversion apps: what each one actually meters

Researched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-01

One of these bills you on the revenue it generates. That is the fairest model here, and the one to model most carefully.

Conversion apps are sold on upside, which makes them unusually easy to install and unusually hard to evaluate. The install is a click; the cost arrives later and in a shape you may not have looked at. The four here meter in four different ways, and only one of them ties its fee to the money it actually makes you.

The second thing worth saying out loud: an urgency widget makes a claim to your shopper. If the stock is not low or the sale does not end, that claim is not a growth tactic, it is a false statement on a product page — and regulators treat it as one.

How we picked

  • We read the app’s own listing and date it, and where the price moved between two readings we say so.
  • We name the meter, because ‘from $X a month’ hides more here than in most categories.
  • We flag where an app makes a claim to your customer, since that carries a risk no pricing page mentions.

Revenue-share is the honest model, and the one to model

Being billed on the revenue a feature produces is the fairest arrangement on this page: no upside, no fee. It is also the one that makes a spreadsheet hard, because the number only exists after the thing works. Take your current attach rate, apply a modest lift, and look at what the fee becomes at that level before you decide it is cheap.

For every other app here, the discipline is simpler and rarely done: measure the conversion difference for a few weeks with the app off. Conversion tooling is the category where belief substitutes for measurement most often, and the apps themselves have no incentive to correct that.

Frequently asked questions

Do urgency widgets actually work?

They can lift conversion when the scarcity is real, because they surface information the shopper wants. When it is manufactured, you are trading a short-term lift for a claim you cannot defend.

Is a revenue-share app more expensive than a flat one?

At low volume, no — you pay almost nothing. At high volume it usually costs more than a flat tier would, which is the trade you accept for paying nothing while it is unproven.

How many conversion apps should a store run?

Fewer than most stores do. Each one adds script weight to the page it is meant to improve, and past a handful the speed cost starts eating the conversion gain.
