IndieMade lists two plans, Essential and Plus, and every plan starts with a 30-day free trial that needs no credit card. Essential costs $15 a month, or $12 a month billed annually. Plus costs $25 a month, or $20 a month billed annually. The page says IndieMade adds no listing fee and no transaction fee on any plan, and that you take payments through your own Stripe, Square or PayPal account, whose processing fees are separate.

Hosting is included and every plan supports a custom domain. Unlimited products are included on every plan. The terms state prices in US dollars. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

Independent online store builder for makers and small sellers, with two-way Etsy sync, unlimited products and no platform transaction fees.

    How to actually use IndieMade

    Start the 30-day trial at indiemade.com, then follow the quick-start guide: set your site name and logo in Basic Site Info, enter your address, open Taxes in the dashboard settings, choose a shipping method, and add at least one payment provider so customers can pay you. If you choose USPS calculated shipping, every product needs a weight and dimensions. We did not open a store ourselves.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from IndieMade?

    Exit check 2026-11-19; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does IndieMade cost?

    Essential costs $15 a month, or $12 a month billed annually. Plus costs $25 a month, or $20 a month billed annually.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes, 30 days, and the page says no credit card is required.

    Does IndieMade take a cut of sales?

    The page says it adds no listing fee and no transaction fee on any plan. Payments go through your own Stripe, Square or PayPal account, which charge their own fees.

    Does it sync with Etsy?

    The supplier describes two-way Etsy sync for listings, inventory, variations, personalized options and photos. We did not test the sync.

    What do we not know?

    The current plan prices, the exact cancellation steps and refund policy, and which plan includes the AI integration, since two help pages disagree.
