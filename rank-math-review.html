Rank Math: what it costs and who should skip it

WordPress SEO plugin with schema, on-page analysis and rank tracking. The free version is generous enough that many sites never need to upgrade; PRO, from about $6.99/month billed annually, earns its keep once you want rank tracking, Content AI credits or many client sites.

What to check before you buy

The free version is so generous that many sites never need to upgrade — PRO mainly earns its keep once you want built-in rank tracking, Content AI credits or to manage many sites. Don't pay until you hit those.

The pricing, as we read it

(read 2026-08-26 on rankmath.com/pricing) Pro is €7.99 a month, Business €24.99 and Agency €54.99, each billed annually and excluding VAT, against list prices of €8.99, €27.99 and €64.99. Pro covers unlimited personal sites and 500 keywords; Business 100 client sites and 10,000 keywords; Agency 500 sites and 50,000.

The euros are not a European quirk of our reading: we checked from a US connection and the storefront still priced in euros, so an American pays in euros too and carries whatever their card charges to convert.

When the paid tier is worth it

Rank Math publishes two prices for every plan and the second one is the one you will actually pay. PRO is €7.99 a month billed annually and renews at €8.99 plus taxes. Business is €24.99 renewing at €27.99.

Agency is €54.99 renewing at €64.99. The renewal step is about 12% on PRO and Business — and 18% on Agency, which is the plan least likely to be watching for it (re-verified 2026-08-17; the storefront served us euros from an EU address).

The word doing the most work on that page is 'unlimited'. PRO gives unlimited websites you own — client sites excluded. So the moment you do any work on a site that is not yours, PRO is not a plan you are allowed to use, and your real entry price is Business at €24.99.

That is 3.1× the advertised figure, and it is a licensing distinction rather than a feature one, which is exactly the kind that gets discovered after the card is charged. If you are staying personal, the keyword meter is what decides the step.

PRO tracks 500 keywords at €7.99, which is €0.016 per keyword a month; Business tracks 10,000 at €24.99, or €0.0025 — six times better. Agency's 50,000 at €54.99 works out at €0.0011, another 2.3× improvement, and its 500 client sites cost €0.11 each against Business's €0.25.

In other words, every step up this ladder is genuinely cheaper per unit, which is unusual and worth knowing: if you are tracking more than 500 keywords, staying on PRO is the expensive choice. The bundle deserves one look.

Rank Math plus WP Rocket is €11.99 renewing at €13.99, so WP Rocket costs €4.00 a month inside the bundle and €5.00 after renewal — a 25% step on that component alone, steeper than either product's own renewal increase. None of which changes the honest headline: the free version is good enough that a lot of sites never need any of this.

PRO earns its keep at the point where you want built-in rank tracking, Content AI credits, or many sites under one licence. Until one of those three is true, the correct plan is the free one.

What the price has done

One to read carefully, because the numbers did not move but the currency did. Our July 2026 capture of Rank Math's pricing page recorded the same set of figures in dollars that the August 2026 capture recorded in euros — identical numerals, different symbol.

That is a storefront switch, not a price change, and it is the kind of thing that makes a tool look cheaper or dearer than it is depending on where you read it from. When we re-read the page on 17 August 2026 it was still quoting euros, with the first-year and renewal prices shown side by side.

How we would start

Install the free version and run it for a full month before you look at a price. It is capable enough that a single site with a reasonable content workflow may never need more. And the three things that justify paying — rank tracking, Content AI credits, many sites on one licence — are all things you will notice the absence of, rather than things you need to test for.

Before you do pay, settle the licensing question in writing if there is any doubt: PRO's 'unlimited websites' means sites you own, and client sites are excluded. If you build or maintain anything for someone else, your entry plan is Business at €24.99, not PRO at €7.99.

Then budget the renewal rather than the first year. PRO renews at €8.99, Business at €27.99, Agency at €64.99 — and note that Agency's step is 18% rather than the 12% the two lower plans take. Count your keywords too: past 500 you are paying six times more per tracked keyword on PRO than you would on Business.

Who it is for

Best for WordPress site owners who want a powerful, feature-rich SEO plugin — the free version alone beats most paid rivals.

Skip it if your SEO needs are basic and the free version covers them — there's no rush to PRO until you need tracking or Content AI.

What to weigh it against

The comparison that matters is Yoast SEO or The SEO Framework — other WordPress SEO plugins.

Questions buyers actually ask

Is the Rank Math and WP Rocket bundle worth it?

It is the single most-searched thing about Rank Math on our pages, so here is the arithmetic as read on 17 August 2026: the bundle is €11.99 a month billed annually, renewing at €13.99, against €7.99 (renewing €8.99) for Rank Math PRO alone. You are therefore paying about €4 a month for the WP Rocket side. Whether that beats buying WP Rocket separately depends on how many sites you run — check WP Rocket's own licence count before you commit, since that is where the value sits or does not.

What does Rank Math cost in year two?

More than year one, and the page says so plainly: PRO renews at €8.99 a month plus taxes against a first-year €7.99, Business at €27.99 against €24.99, and Agency at €64.99 against €54.99. All are billed annually. This is the most common complaint about Rank Math in reviews, and it is not hidden — it is on the pricing page, under the price.

Does PRO really cover unlimited websites?

Unlimited websites that you own. Client websites are explicitly excluded, and that is the line that decides which tier a freelancer needs: Business at €24.99 covers 100 client sites and Agency at €54.99 covers 500. If you build for other people, PRO is not the plan regardless of how few sites you have.
