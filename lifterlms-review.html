Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 22 Nov 2026

(read 2026-10-10 on lifterlms.com/pricing, USD) The LifterLMS core plugin for WordPress is free and open source, with unlimited courses, memberships, students and teachers. Paid bundles are annual subscriptions, shown at an introductory 50% off the first year with the code FIRSTYEAR50, and the page states that all renewals are at full price. Earth is $199 for the first year, normally $398: 1 active site, support for 1 user, the Stripe, PayPal and WooCommerce e-commerce add-ons, cart abandonment recovery and name-your-price.

Universe, marked most popular, is $299 for the first year, normally $598: 5 active sites, support for 2 users, the Sky Pilot theme, 4 e-commerce add-ons, email marketing and CRM add-ons (including Mailchimp and Kit), form integrations (Gravity Forms, Ninja Forms, Formidable Forms, WPForms) and a weekly mastermind with a private Facebook group. Infinity is $799 for the first year, normally $1,598: unlimited sites, support for 3 users and 15 advanced add-ons such as advanced quizzes and videos, assignments, groups, private areas, a private site and social learning.

A lifetime Infinity licence is offered on a separate page. A 30-day demo site with every add-on costs $1, and purchases carry a 30-day, 100% money-back guarantee with no setup fees. Licences renew automatically; you can cancel the renewal at any time. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What LifterLMS does

WordPress plugin for building and selling online courses and memberships, with a free open-source core and paid add-ons for payments, email marketing, quizzes and groups.

The plugin is free; the payment add-ons are what you buy

The free core plugin runs a complete course site: courses, memberships, quizzes and students without limits. What it lacks is a way to take money, because the Stripe, PayPal and WooCommerce add-ons sit in the paid bundles. So for most course sellers the realistic starting point is Earth: $199 in the first year and $398 a year after that, for one site. Universe at $598 a year from year two makes sense if you need email or CRM integrations, form plugins or more than one site.

Infinity at $1,598 a year from year two is for course businesses that need assignments, groups, private areas or social learning, or run many sites. Budget on the renewal price, not the first-year price: the page says renewals are always at full price.

How to actually use LifterLMS

Install the free core plugin on a WordPress site and build one course before buying anything. If you need checkout, decide between Earth and Universe by the integrations you use (email, CRM, forms) and the number of sites. The $1 demo site with every add-on for 30 days is the cheapest way to test the advanced add-ons before paying for Infinity. Note the renewal date: the first year is half price and the renewal is charged at full price automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How much does LifterLMS cost?

Read on 10 October 2026: the core plugin is free; Earth $199 for the first year then $398 a year, Universe $299 then $598, Infinity $799 then $1,598.

Can I sell courses with the free LifterLMS plugin?

The free core runs courses and memberships, but the Stripe, PayPal and WooCommerce payment add-ons are part of the paid bundles.

Does LifterLMS renew at the discounted price?

No. The pricing page says the 50% discount applies to the first year and all renewals are at full price.

What happens to my LifterLMS site if I stop renewing?

It keeps working. The FAQ on the pricing page, read 22 November 2026, says LifterLMS and every add-on are open source and do not stop functioning when a licence lapses. What stops is add-on updates, security upgrades and support access. Licences renew automatically, and you can cancel the scheduled renewal at any time.

Do staging and test sites count against the LifterLMS site limit?

No. The same FAQ says only live sites count: Earth covers 1, Universe up to 5 and Infinity unlimited, while staging, development and test sites are unlimited on every plan. You move a licence by deactivating it on one live site and activating it on another. Read 22 November 2026.

Can my developer get LifterLMS support without access to billing?

Yes, through support accounts: 1 on Earth, 2 on Universe and 3 on Infinity. The comparison table, read 22 November 2026, says these extra people can view your downloads and raise tickets on your behalf, but cannot buy, change renewals or see your payment details.
