We tested it · 2026-10-21

Tested by our team on 21 October 2026 on the free Pay As You Go plan, without a card and without connecting a mailbox: Daan created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in Daan's browser. We described a made-up bicycle repair workshop in four fields, with three hard facts (repairs only, no bike sales; open Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00; no appointments by phone), and checked what the onboarding built from it.

How we tested

  1. Completed the business-profile onboarding
  2. Checked the facts in the generated profile
  3. Examined proposed leads and their email sequence
  4. Inspected the content plan and a draft blog post
  5. Explored the AI team and website agents
  6. Read the credit and plan controls
  • The whole onboarding, which built a company profile, an ideal customer profile, an AI team, a knowledge base, website agents, first leads, an email sequence, a content plan and sample content in about two minutes, cost 4 credits in 14 requests (Company Search 7 requests / 2 credits, Content Engine 6 / 2, Sequence 1 / 0). The General page then read 'Monthly credits 4 / 50'.
  • Of our three facts, the profile kept one ('without the sale of retail bicycles'), blurred one into 'standard weekday business hours' and replaced the third with something we never said: 'By operating as a walk-in facility'. It also set the industry to 'Transportation'.
  • Without asking, it built a list of 16 real people (facility and operations managers at large companies in Amsterdam) around a partnership idea of its own, and enrolled all 16 in a five-email, AI-personalised sequence. The screen says the sequence 'stays paused until you connect a mailbox and turn on sending'; we connected no mailbox and nothing was sent. We did not record the names.

Parallel AI now sells flat plans with usage limits instead of credits. Starter costs $29 a month, or $24 a month billed yearly, with a 7-day free trial: unlimited AI work and leads under fair use, lead search and enrichment from 300M+ contacts, AI employees, Smart Lists, Sequences and the Content Engine, 1 LinkedIn account and 3 branded domains. Entrepreneur costs $119 a month, or $99 a month billed yearly, for 6 times the usage of Starter, 2 LinkedIn accounts, 5 branded domains, a white-label instance and the App Builder.

Business costs $356 a month, or $297 a month billed yearly, for 30 times the usage of Starter, 10 LinkedIn accounts, 10 branded domains, shared AI employees and priority support. Enterprise is custom, with SAML SSO, on-premise deployment and dedicated support. Every plan includes full API and MCP access. The page says every plan is unlimited for normal business use, that usage refreshes on a rolling 5-hour window so a heavy session may need a short pause, and that sustained automated use far beyond normal business activity may be limited for the rest of the month.

Add-ons: an extra white-label instance $50 a month, an extra branded domain $5, a cold-email mailbox $5 each, an extra LinkedIn account $25 and an extra static IP $25. There is no longer a free plan; the old Pay As You Go tier with 50 credits does not appear on the page. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Every plan is called unlimited for normal business use, but usage refreshes on a rolling 5-hour window and sustained heavy automation may be limited for the rest of the month, so heavy users cannot tell in advance where Starter stops and Entrepreneur starts. The free plan is gone; there is a 7-day trial on Starter instead. Cold-email mailboxes and domains are paid add-ons on every plan.

What Parallel AI does

AI agent platform for sales, marketing and support: build AI employees on every major model, run Smart Lists for lead generation and enrichment, and schedule content and support agents from one place.

Usage multiples, not features, set the tier

Starter already includes the full feature set for one person: AI employees, lead search, sequences, the Content Engine, websites and the API. The upgrades mainly buy usage headroom (6 times Starter on Entrepreneur, 30 times on Business), more LinkedIn accounts and branded domains, and on Entrepreneur a white-label instance, which costs $50 a month as an add-on on Starter. Business adds sharing AI employees across a team and priority support.

The page does not define the Starter usage limit in numbers; it describes fair use with a rolling 5-hour window, so the multiples are relative to a baseline you only learn by working with it.

What we found running Parallel AI ourselves

Hands-on · 2026-10-21

The first findings are at the top of this page. The rest:

  • The content plan, in three funnel stages, promised things we never said: 'Our same-day repair policy explained', 'Success stories: Keeping busy local families mobile' and a 'high-speed repair experience'. Our opening hours and the phone rule appeared nowhere, and the blog draft targeted 'bicycle repair shop' at 22,200 estimated searches a month in the US, for a Dutch audience.
  • It proposed six AI employees (sales, support, an appointment scheduler and SEO among them) and two chat widgets, for sales and support, each with a ready-made script snippet to paste into a site. With no real website given, the knowledge base reported 'We couldn't import pages from your website this time'.
  • Right after onboarding a subscription screen opened. On its annual billing tab: Pay As You Go $0 (1 company, 1 seat, 50 credits a month), Entrepreneur $99 a month ($1,188 a year, 2 companies, 2,000 credits), Business $297 a month ($3,564 a year, 10 companies, 10 seats, 9,000 credits) and Enterprise on request. The free plan showed 'Data credits per 5-hour window 0 / 0'.

How to actually use Parallel AI

Start the 7-day free trial on Starter at parallellabs.app. Use it on the workflow you would actually buy this for: describe your business so the AI employees learn it, build a Smart List of leads, and draft a sequence or a content plan. Watch whether the rolling 5-hour usage window ever makes you pause; if it does on normal work, that is the signal you need Entrepreneur's 6 times the usage.

What our own test on 21 October 2026 adds (then on the free Pay As You Go plan, which no longer exists): the onboarding builds a profile, leads, an email sequence and a content plan. Read what it generated before you use it, because it invented claims about our test business (a walk-in facility, a same-day repair policy, success stories). And look at the lead list before you connect a mailbox: it had already enrolled 16 real people it found in a five-email sequence that only waits for sending to be turned on.
