Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 19 Nov 2026

Four monthly plans: Starter $39/mo (50 unique / 25 premium products), Professional $59/mo (500 unique / 250 premium), Empire $99/mo (5,000 unique / 2,500 premium) and Unicorn $299/mo (25,000 unique / 25,000 premium). A 7-day trial costs $1, and there are no transaction or hidden fees. Annual billing is listed only from Professional up, $288, $684 and $948 a year, and the discount grows with the tier: 6 months off Professional, 5 off Empire and 8 off Unicorn, so the advertised “up to 8 months off” is the Unicorn rate rather than a general one, and Starter has no annual column at all.

An optional one-time custom-store build service starts at $299. The $1 trial converts to the full monthly rate of whichever plan you picked, and your card stays on file, set a reminder if you are only testing. Read again on 17 November 2026: the four monthly amounts, the $1 for 7 days trial on any monthly plan, the no order fees line, the yearly tab marked up to 8 months off and the custom store build from $299 one time were unchanged; the page now also shows a slider for the number of products (50, 500, 5,000 and 25,000).

AliDrop has no listing on the Shopify App Store that we could find, so there is no listing to read. Re-read 19 November 2026 on alidrop.co/pricing, including the plan data behind the yearly toggle: $39, $59, $99 and $299 a month, yearly only from Professional up at $24, $57 and $79 a month ($288, $684 and $948 billed yearly), Starter monthly only, custom store from $299; unchanged. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

When the paid plan is worth it

Alidrop has no free tier: the only entry point is a $1, 7-day trial, after which every plan is paid, so the real question is which tier, not whether. The catalog-size ceiling is what forces the decision: Starter caps you at 50 unique products, Professional at 500, Empire at 5,000, and Unicorn at 25,000. Starter pays for itself only while you are validating a handful of products; the moment you run more than roughly 50 live SKUs, or want to scale a store that is already selling, you hit the wall and a higher tier becomes unavoidable.

Paying up buys headroom, not capability: the tiers list the same winning-products access, product analyzer, and chat support, so upgrading raises quota, not features.

How to actually use Alidrop

First value comes from installing the Alidrop app from the Shopify App Store (or the WooCommerce connector), linking your AliExpress account, then importing products: either through the dashboard or a Chrome extension that pushes items one at a time while you browse supplier sites. You edit titles, images, and variants, publish to your store, and Alidrop places the supplier order and syncs tracking. Budget your first session for setup and reviewing each listing before you publish, not just clicking import.

Settle the billing term before the $1 trial rolls over: read on their pricing page on 13 September and again on 2 October 2026, annual billing is offered only from Professional up ($288, $684 and $948 a year), so the $39 Starter plan is month to month and the advertised up-to-8-months-off applies to the $299 Unicorn tier alone. The same page describes the trial as Starter-level access (product importing, order processing and AliExpress tools) so test the supplier source you actually plan to sell from inside those seven days.

Frequently asked questions

What happens after the $1 trial?

It becomes the full price of the plan you chose ($39, $59, $99 or $299 a month) seven days after you start, and the card you used stays on file. Those figures were on the page on 17 August and again on 2 October 2026 and in our July and August captures before that. The billing complaints on review platforms cluster on exactly this moment, so diary it before you sign up.

Which plan do I actually need?

The plans differ by catalogue size, not by feature class: 50 unique and 25 premium products on Starter, 500 and 250 on Professional, 5,000 and 2,500 on Empire, and 25,000 of each on Unicorn. Pick on the number of products you will really list, and note that annual billing is advertised as up to eight months off.

Does Alidrop keep supplier prices in sync?

No, and this is the operational risk rather than the sticker price. There is no automatic price sync, so a supplier raising their price does not raise yours: it eats your margin on every order until you notice. If you run thin margins, build a manual price check into your week.

Can I get money back if I forget to cancel, or only uninstall the app?

No on both counts, per Alidrop's refund policy (alidrop.co/refund-policy, read 19 November 2026). The subscription renews unless you cancel in your account settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date, and there are no partial or prorated refunds for unused time, mid-cycle cancellations or inactive months. Cancelling inside the 7-day trial means no charge for the monthly plan; after that, monthly fees are not refunded. Annual plans have no trial and are non-refundable once paid. Uninstalling Alidrop from your store does not cancel the subscription. The pricing page sells the trial as "7 days for $1" while the policy calls it a free trial; whether that $1 comes back is not confirmed.

Do higher Alidrop plans add features, or only product slots?

The vendor's own page gives two answers. Read 19 November 2026, the pricing cards say "Every plan includes" winning products, one-click import, order automation, tracking sync, US & EU suppliers and VIP chat support, and the four tiers differ by product count (50, 500, 5,000 and 25,000). The FAQ on the same page says Professional and Empire "add more products, AI tools and priority support" and that advanced plans add bulk order processing. Which AI tools sit behind which tier is not listed, so if bulk ordering is the reason to upgrade, confirm it in the $1 week before you pay for a higher plan.
