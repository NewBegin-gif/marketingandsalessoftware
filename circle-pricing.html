Circle pricing: what checkout actually shows (tested 31 Oct 2026)

We opened Circle’s own checkout on 31 Oct 2026 for Professional, annual preselected and stopped before paying: no card entered, nothing bought. Below is what the pricing page says, what the checkout screen showed, and what that means for your bill.

  • Amount due today$1,068 for Professional, billed annually
  • Monthly equivalent$89/mo billed annually; monthly billing is $129/mo
  • Billing termAnnual billing was preselected

What the pricing page says

“$89/mo” (read 31 Oct 2026 on circle.so)

What the checkout showed (31 Oct 2026)

Amount due today$1,068 for Professional, billed annually
Monthly equivalent$89/mo billed annually; monthly billing is $129/mo
Billing termAnnual billing was preselected
TaxUnknown. No tax line was visible
CurrencyUSD
Auto-renewalNot measured
Card needed for the trialNo (pricing page: "No credit card required")

“Unknown” means the screen did not show it before card details; it is not zero. Amounts are exactly as displayed, in the currency the checkout used.

What that means for you

  • $89/mo is $1,068 charged up front for the year, not a monthly bill.
  • Annual is preselected; the monthly price for the same plan is $129/mo.
  • No tax line was visible, so the final amount is unknown.

Screenshot

Who it is not for

Not for buyers who want to pay month by month at the advertised price, or who need a tax-inclusive total before committing to a year.

How the pricing page moved

We re-read Circle’s pricing page every month, 2 Aug 2026 to 2 Nov 2026 (4 checks). The text of the pricing page changed between 3 of 3 monthly checks; the most recent change happened between 1 Oct 2026 and 2 Nov 2026. Our monthly check compares the whole page text, so a change can be a new testimonial as well as a new price; the checkout figures above are what we saw on the test date.
