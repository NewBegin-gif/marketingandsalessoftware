What the price did

We read Omniseo’s own pricing page on a schedule and keep every reading, so this is our record of the page. It is not a forecast, and not someone else’s summary. A change appears here only after we confirmed the new price on the page itself.

Worth knowing how long the offer has been running: our July and August 2026 captures of Omniseo's pricing page are byte-for-byte identical. Both already carried the 'Limited-Time Offer: try the full platform for just $45 (50% off)' banner, along with the same $89, $349 and $899+ tiers. It was still there when we re-read the page on 17 August 2026. A limited-time offer that has not moved in six weeks is effectively the standard first-month price. On 28 September 2026 the tiers were unchanged ($89, $349, $899+; $890 and $3,490 a year) but the 'Limited-Time Offer' banner had been dropped: the half-price first month is now described simply as what follows the 7-day trial, which confirms it was the standard first-month price all along. Read once more on 20 October 2026: the same three tiers and the same $890 and $3,490 annual figures. What the page now spells out is the scope of the trial, ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews only, for seven days, which is narrower than either paid plan.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Tracking how you appear in ChatGPT and other AI answers is useful signal, but the metrics are far less standardized than classic SEO, and you're paying to monitor a moving target you only partly influence. And the step from Essentials to Professional is a big jump for more channels and prompts. Treat it as an early-mover intelligence tool, not a guaranteed ranking lever. Two smaller cautions from the pricing page itself, read on 20 October 2026.

The trial covers two engines for seven days, so it cannot show you what the four- or ten-engine plans report. And support is email-only on both self-serve plans; chat and phone start at Enterprise. The crawler analysis the site promotes works from server logs and crawl events, and the pricing table does not say which plan includes it.

When the paid plan is worth it

OmniSEO's split runs between paid tiers rather than free-vs-paid: there is no self-serve free tier for the core platform, only separate standalone tools. Essentials, at $89/month ($45 for the first month), suits a single-brand team tracking a handful of AI channels: it caps at 4 AI channels and 50 saved prompts per month, with no CSV/PDF export. Teams needing more channels or heavier prompt research push toward Professional at $349/month ($175 first month), which raises the caps to 10 AI channels, 100 saved prompts per month, and adds 5,000-row exports per month.

What upgrading does not solve is competitor visibility: both Essentials and Professional track only 5 competitors: that ceiling only lifts on Enterprise, priced from $899+/month with custom quotes, which lists "5 per brand/serviceline" instead of a flat cap.

Same price 4 months running

Our monthly capture read this vendor's pricing page every month from July 2026 to October 2026, and the amounts were the same all 4 times, including $349, $890, $899 and $3,490 from the pricing above. We keep watching, and the registry records what moves.

How to actually use Omniseo

There are two entry points: self-serve signup at app.omniseo.com/signup (Essentials or Professional, discounted to $45 or $175 for the first month), or a demo request, which requires name, email, website URL, and a message, with only the company field optional. OmniSEO states its team configures tracking on your behalf, "our team sets everything up for you", rather than a self-service dashboard you configure yourself, so first value depends on their setup pace rather than an instant API connection.

For the demo path, OmniSEO commits to following up within 24 hours to schedule the session, not to activating tracking immediately. Because setup is vendor-managed, there is no published documentation describing how domain or channel verification actually works, which makes it hard to judge activation time in advance. On Essentials, the 50 ad-hoc query lookups included per month can also be used up quickly while still testing which prompts and channels matter.

Before the trial starts, write down three lists, because the plan caps count against them: the competitors you want benchmarked (five on both Essentials and Professional), the prompts you want monitored (50 or 100 a month), and the engines your buyers actually use. Spend the seven trial days on ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews only, see which of your pages are already cited there, and only then decide whether the six engines Professional adds (Gemini, Copilot, Claude, Meta AI, Grok and DeepSeek) are worth $260 a month more.

If crawler data is part of the reason you are buying, ask before subscribing how server-log access is connected; no setup documentation for it is published.

Frequently asked questions

What does Omniseo cost?

Essentials $89 a month, Professional $349, Enterprise from $899 with custom terms. Annual billing is $890 and $3,490 a year, which the page calls two months free. Read on 17 August 2026 and unchanged on 28 September 2026.

Is the 50%-off first month a real offer?

It is real: you pay $45 rather than $89 (or $175 rather than $349) for the first paid month after the 7-day trial. Our July and August 2026 captures showed it under a 'Limited-Time Offer' banner; by 28 September 2026 that banner was gone and the pricing page and its structured data present it as the standard sequence, 'Free 7-day trial, then 50% off your first paid month'. Budget for the full rate from month two.

What separates Essentials from Professional?

Coverage rather than depth: Essentials tracks 4 AI channels with 50 prompts and 5 seats, Professional the full set of 10 channels with 100 prompts. If you only care about visibility in one or two assistants, the four-channel tier is the honest fit and the jump to $349 buys breadth you may not use.

Which AI engines does each Omniseo plan watch?

The comparison table on omniseo.com/pricing, read on 20 October 2026, names them. Essentials covers four: Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity. Professional adds Gemini, Copilot, Claude, Meta AI, Grok and DeepSeek for the full set of ten. Enterprise is listed as '10+' with custom regions and model cohorts. The free trial is narrower than both paid plans: ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews only, so Perplexity and AI Mode results cannot be seen before the first payment.

How many ad-hoc prompt lookups are included?

These are separate from saved prompts. Saved prompts are monitored continuously (50 a month on Essentials, 100 on Professional); lookups are one-off checks of how often a prompt is searched. The table read on 20 October 2026 gives 50 lookups a month on Essentials, 200 on Professional and '2,000+/mo (fair-use; queued)' on Enterprise. Professional quadruples lookups while only doubling saved prompts, so prompt research is where the $260 monthly gap between the two plans shows most.

Can I get money back if I cancel part-way through?

Do not count on it. OmniSEO's footer says the product is powered by WebFX and links to the RevenueCloudFX terms of WebpageFX. Those terms, read on 20 October 2026, make the customer responsible for an 'effective and authenticated' cancellation notice and say WebpageFX is not required to give a prorated refund of services paid. The pricing FAQ adds that plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. On the $3,490 annual Professional plan that combination matters far more than on monthly billing.

How does Omniseo compare with cheaper AI-visibility trackers?

On the vendors' own pricing pages: Superlines' Starter is 79 € a month for 50 tracked prompts, one brand and three answer engines you pick (read 16 October 2026); CiteRoute's Pro is $79 a month for 10 monitored prompts plus a dashboard of AI crawler visits from a tracking tag (read 13 October 2026). Omniseo's $89 Essentials matches Superlines on prompt count, fixes the engines at four including both Google surfaces, and gives five seats. Its gap at that price is exports: CSV and PDF start on the $349 plan.
